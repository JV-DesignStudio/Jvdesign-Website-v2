## Animation/Video Editing Notes

### Transitions to Use:
- **Redesign reveal**: Wipe or spin transition showing old → new
- **Feature showcase**: Zoom in on key elements
- **Progress**: Fill animation for XP bar
- **Completion**: Confetti/celebration when level up
- **Learning flow**: Smooth scroll through steps

### Music/Audio Suggestions:
- Upbeat, modern background music (royalty-free)
- Sound effects: 
  - "Ding" for level up
  - "Woosh" for transitions
  - "Success" for challenge completion

### Video Specs:

**TikTok:**
- Format: 1080x1920 (vertical)
- Duration: 15-60 seconds
- File: MP4, H.264
- Aspect ratio: 9:16

**YouTube Shorts:**
- Format: 1080x1920 (vertical)
- Duration: 15-60 seconds
- File: MP4
- Max file size: 4GB

**Instagram Reels:**
- Format: 1080x1920 (vertical)
- Duration: 15-90 seconds
- File: MP4
- Aspect ratio: 9:16

**Instagram Feed Post:**
- Format: 1080x1350
- File: MP4/JPG
- Aspect ratio: 4:5

**Twitter/X Post:**
- Format: 1024x512 (landscape)
- File: JPG/PNG/MP4
- Max video: 2 min 20 sec

## Performance Metrics to Track

### What to Monitor:
- Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares)
- Click-through rate to website
- Follower growth rate
- Video view duration
- Share counts

### Target Metrics:
- Instagram posts: 200+ likes, 10+ comments
- TikTok/Shorts: 1000+ views, 50+ shares
- Twitter: 50+ likes, 10+ retweets
- LinkedIn: 30+ likes, 5+ comments

## A/B Testing Ideas

1. **Caption style**: Question vs. Statement vs. Storytelling
2. **Visual**: Screenshot vs. Graphic vs. Video
3. **CTA**: "Learn more" vs. "Start now" vs. "Try free"
4. **Posting time**: Test morning, afternoon, evening
5. **Hashtag count**: Test 5 vs. 15 vs. 30 hashtags

## Accessibility Checklist

- [ ] All images have alt text
- [ ] Videos have captions/subtitles
- [ ] Color contrast is sufficient (WCAG AA)
- [ ] Text overlays are readable on all backgrounds
- [ ] Links are clearly visible
- [ ] No flashing/strobing elements in videos

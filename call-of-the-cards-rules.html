Call of the Cards: Quest for the Crown

Rules v1.3 - 108 cards (46/deck ×2 +16 quests) - 2 players · 20-40 min · First to 5 VP

← Play digitally · Markdown ·

Page 1 - Setup (30 seconds)

Each player: Shuffle 46-card deck, draw 5. Gold stays in hand (your bank).
Centre: Shuffle 16 quests, reveal 3. Put deck nearby, leave discard piles.
Start: Player who last read a book goes first. First player already drew 1 (starts 6).

  • Gold 💰 - Currency. Discard Gold cards from hand to pay.
  • Companion 🤝 - Power to claim quests. Cost & Power in corners.
  • Artifact 💎 - Permanent bonus, stays in front of you.
  • Quest 📜 - Centre, requires Power, gives VP.

Page 2 - Your Turn (in order)

1. Draw

Draw 1 (2 if you have Amulet of Renewal). Underdog's Resolve (v1.3): If you're 2+ VP behind at start of turn, draw +1 extra. Stacks with Amulet (up to 3).

2. Main actions (any order)

Recruit - Pay Gold by discarding Gold cards, put companion in Party.
Acquire - Same for artifacts.
Complete Quest - If total Power ≥ quest requirement, claim it, gain VP, replace centre quest.
You may Discard 1 to cycle. End Turn to pass.

Power: Sum companion Power + bonuses: Ring 4g +1 all, Healer 2g/2p +1 all (stacks: 2 Healers+Ring=+3 each). Shield 4g quests -1 Power. Underdog is draw, not Power.

Page 4 - Strategy & Print

Early: Rogue/Scout → Mid: Captain/Healer+Ring → Late: Siege/Archer+Sword. Don't buy Sword before 3VP. Time Thief when opponent has 6-7 cards.

Print: Punch 46+46+16 + VP track (0-5) or 10 tokens. Sleeve Gold. Print companions white, artifacts gold tint, quests parchment. 108 = 2×54 sheets.

✦ The Story

A tiny speck with a
universe of heart

Stardust is small the smallest thing in the cosmos. But as it floats through nebulae, past planets and moons, it slowly discovers that being small doesn't mean being unimportant. Every star began as stardust, after all.

Written with lyrical, gentle prose and brought to life with deep, glowing illustrations, this is the perfect bedtime book to help little ones feel big and spark their very first questions about the universe above.

✦ A Note from the Author

Why I wrote this story

I've always been fascinated by the idea that everything in the universe including us is made of stardust. I wanted to find a way to share that wonder with the youngest readers, and help them feel connected to something truly enormous.

Stardust's journey is a reminder that size doesn't determine worth. Every child, like every speck of stardust, is part of something incredible.

✦ The Story

What is this book about?

A tiny speck of stardust drifts alone through the universe. It passes galaxies, nebulae, spinning planets — each one vast and full of things that know exactly what they are. But stardust is small. Too small to be a star, too scattered to be a planet.

Then it lands somewhere unexpected. And everything changes. A quiet, poetic book for the youngest readers about belonging, identity, and the surprising places we find our place in the world.

For parents & teachers

For the littlest readers

Stardust is the youngest-facing book in the JVDesignStudio collection, written for ages 2–6 with simple, rhythmic text and lots of space on each page. It works as a lap book — something read slowly with a child tucked beside you, with time to look at the illustrations and talk about what stardust might be feeling.

The belonging theme speaks to children starting nursery or school for the first time, children who have recently moved, or any child who has ever felt like they don't quite fit in yet.

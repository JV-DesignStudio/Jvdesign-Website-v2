✦ What I Believe

The values behind every project

💜
Brave Doesn't Have to Be Loud
The quietest kind of courage is still courage. Resting, adapting, and trying a different way are all forms of strength and stories should show that.
✨
Every Heart Has a Spark
No matter what someone is carrying, their inner light can't be put out. Every book I make is built on that belief for the child reading it, and the grown-up alongside them.
🎨
Design Is Care Made Visible
The way something looks and feels is part of the story. When a page is beautiful, when a game feels right in your hands that's intentional love made tangible.
🌱
Small Tries Count
You don't have to climb the whole mountain today. One small step, honestly taken, matters in stories and in life. This studio was built that way too.
✦ The Studio Story

How JVDesignStudio came to be

The Beginning
A love of stories and making things
JVDesignStudio started not with a business plan but with a feeling that the best things are made slowly, carefully, and with a genuine reason to exist. The first ideas lived in notebooks before they ever became pages.
First Books
Elara, Stardust, and Pip arrive
The first wave of picture books stories about online safety, cosmic curiosity, and facing the dark. Each one was an experiment in finding a voice that was warm without being soft, and honest without being heavy.
Games & Experiments
Tabletop games, digital prototypes, free kits
The studio expanded into games cooperative card games, tabletop RPGs for children, digital runners, and free creative kits. Each project pushed into new territory while staying anchored in the same values.
Personal Work
Lumo and the Grumble-Grit
After years of learning how to carry their own Grumble-Grit, J. Valentine created Lumo the most personal book yet. A story not about fixing hard things, but about learning to live alongside them with quiet courage.
Now
Echo, new projects, and what's next
Echo and the Mountain of Choices is in illustration. Five more books are in development. The studio is growing one small try at a time.

Meet Stardust

A tiny golden speck drifting through the cosmos, wondering if it matters at all — until it discovers it belongs to everything.

This game is inspired by Stardust's Cosmic Adventure — a picture book about finding your place in the universe. Guide Stardust through nebulae, gather light, and discover that even the smallest things can become something extraordinary.

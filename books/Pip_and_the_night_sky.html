✦ The Story

Brave in the sunshine.
Learning to be brave at night.

Pip is a turtle who loves the daytime bold, bright and fearless in the sun. But when the light fades and shadows stretch along the hall, even the bravest turtle feels small.

With the gentle guidance of his father and a little lantern called the Brave Light, Pip discovers that the Great Dark isn't hiding something terrible it's hiding something wonderful. A perfect bedtime book for any child who finds the dark a little too quiet.

✦ A Note from the Author

Why Pip needed to exist

So many children fear the dark and so many adults have forgotten what that really feels like. I wanted to write a story that didn't rush past the fear, but sat with it, acknowledged it, and then gently walked through it hand in hand.

The shadow that turns out to be Pip's own, the lantern that stays with him rather than going ahead, the stars that only appear when the lights go low these felt like the truest ways to show a child that courage isn't about not being scared. It's about going anyway.

J. Valentine, JVDesignStudio 🌙

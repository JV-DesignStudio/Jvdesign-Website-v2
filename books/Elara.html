✦ The Story

What is this book about?

Elara loves her tablet the games, the videos, the glowing world behind the glass. But one day something slips through: a shadowy figure called the Snatcher, who collects children's secrets and sells them to the highest bidder.

To stop him, Elara must navigate the Wire-Web, a tangled digital maze full of false promises and locked doors, and find the one key that the Snatcher doesn't know exists. A modern fairy tale about online safety that feels like an adventure, not a lecture.

For parents & teachers

Starting the online safety conversation

Elara was written to give parents and teachers a story-shaped way into conversations about online safety with young children, without using scary statistics or abstract warnings that don't land with under-8s.

The Wire-Web is a metaphor children understand instinctively. The Snatcher isn't a scary monster; he's charming and persuasive, which is more honest and more useful. After reading, children naturally want to talk about what they would and wouldn't share online.

## 🖼️ Visual Style Guide

### Color Palette:
- **Primary**: Rose (#BC4749) - for achievements/XP
- **Secondary**: Teal (#70A3A7) - for progression
- **Accent**: Beige (#F0EAD6) - for highlights
- **Dark**: Charcoal (#403B33) - backgrounds
- **Success**: Emerald (#6BCB77) - completion

### Typography:
- **Headlines**: Fredoka 700 (bold game-like feel)
- **Body**: Inter 400-600 (clean, readable)
- **Numbers**: Fredoka 700 (make stats pop)

### Spacing:
- Cards: 24px padding, 20px gaps
- Hero sections: 60px+ top/bottom padding
- Text overlay: Min 40px from edges

### Shadows:
- Cards: `0 4px 16px rgba(64,59,51,0.08)`
- Hover: `0 12px 32px rgba(64,59,51,0.15)`
- Overlays: Dark with 0.6 opacity

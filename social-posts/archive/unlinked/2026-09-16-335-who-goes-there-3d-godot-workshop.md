🛡️ New Workshop: Who Goes There?

Build a real 3D co-op game in Godot 4 - together with your child (ages 8 and up).

Visitors walk up to your checkpoint. You scan their temperature, check for signs of illness, and decide: open the gate or turn them away. Get it wrong and the checkpoint fails.

Part 1 (10 steps): your first 3D scene, walking around in 3D, connecting two players over a local network, the scanning mechanic, gate levers.

Part 2 (7 steps): a shared coin bank, daily rent, rules that get harder every day, a magnifying glass tool, and a win or lose ending on day 10.

Free. No account. Works on any computer that runs Godot 4. Progress saves so you can pick it up across sessions.

Find both parts in the Godot section of the Workshops page.

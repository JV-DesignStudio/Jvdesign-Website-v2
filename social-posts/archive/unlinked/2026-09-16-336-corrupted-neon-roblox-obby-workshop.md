🔮 New Workshop: CORRUPTED

Build a Roblox obby where the level destroys itself behind you.

Platforms fade out and disappear as you run past them. A neon glitch wall closes in from behind. If it catches you, your screen flickers purple and cyan and you respawn at the last checkpoint.

Episode 9 of My First Roblox Studio Game. 8 steps, intermediate Lua:

- CollectionService tags - one script for every platform automatically
- TweenService - smooth platform fade and wall movement
- RemoteEvents - server wall talks to your LocalScript screen effect
- RunService.Heartbeat + delta time - frame-rate-safe movement
- ScreenGui flickering effect in a LocalScript

The server/client split (why the wall and the screen effect need different scripts) is the key concept this workshop is built around. It trips up a lot of Roblox developers and this workshop wires it into the mechanic so you learn it naturally.

Free, no account needed, works in any browser. Progress saves so you can pick it up across sessions.

Find it in the Roblox section of the Workshops page.

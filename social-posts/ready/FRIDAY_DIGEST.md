## The best bits

### CORRUPTED: a Roblox workshop where the level destroys itself
New workshop · 16 September 2026

The ninth Roblox build asks a new question: will the level still be there when you reach it? Platforms vanish as you leave them and a neon wall closes in behind you. Starts from an empty scene and ends with a course you can share.

Link: https://jvdesignstudio.co.uk/devlog#post-336

### Who Goes There? Build a 3D co-op game in Godot
New workshop · 16 September 2026

A two-part Godot 4 project for ages 8 and up, built with a grown-up. Scan visitors, manage the gate and survive ten days, from an empty 3D scene to a game you can play together.

Link: https://jvdesignstudio.co.uk/devlog#post-335

Studio update · 8 September 2026

Improved · 7 September 2026

### Safer offline updates
Improved · 7 September 2026

Link: https://jvdesignstudio.co.uk/devlog#post-83

Lumo Dash

Watch the glowing pattern, then copy it move for move across 6 courses. Chain left, jump, slide, right and dash to earn stars as Lumo the fox.

6 Named Courses
Play First Glow through to the Glow Finale. Patterns grow from 3 moves to 7, and hazard gates mark the tricky beats.

How to Play

1
Pick a Course, Start with First Glow (3 moves) and work up to the Glow Finale (7 moves).
2
Read the Pattern, Watch the beats light up in order on the board before you move.
3
Copy Each Move, Tap the matching buttons in order. A wrong move breaks the pattern.
4
Earn Stars, Finish with stars to spare, build streaks, and beat your saved best.

Pro Tips

👀
Watch First: Read the whole pattern before you start tapping, it only gets longer
🔥
Streaks Score: Clean runs in a row build a streak bonus, one mistake resets it
🌟
Guard Your Stars: Each course gives you 3 stars, mistakes cost them so play carefully
📊
Best Saves: Your best score is stored on this device, chase it with the replay medals

About This Game

Lumo Dash: Glow Courses is a pattern-memory game starring Lumo the fox. Six named courses show glowing movement patterns that you copy with on-screen buttons, from 3-move starters to the 7-move Glow Finale. Hazard gates, star ratings, streak scoring and replay medals keep every run worth repeating. No install, no account, just watch, copy, and glow.

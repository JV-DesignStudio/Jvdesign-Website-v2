Lumo Dash

Jump, slide, and dash through a colorful endless runner! Collect coins, dodge obstacles, grab power-ups, and see how far you can go as Lumo the fox.

🏃 Endless Runner
⚙️ Skill-Based
🎨 3 Biomes
✨ Play Now
🎮 Mode: Endless Runner
💰 Collectibles: Coins & Gems
🛡️ Power-ups: Magnet, Shield, Coin Rush
🌅
3 Beautiful Biomes
Run through Dusk, Night, and Dawn environments with smooth color transitions and unique obstacles in each.
✨
Collectibles
Gather coins and gems to boost your score. Find gems worth 5 points each and unlock coin rush multipliers.
🛡️
Power-ups
Grab Magnet (pulls coins), Shield (absorbs hits), or Coin Rush (2x value) to turn the tables on obstacles.
🎯
Skill Mechanics
Master jumping, double-jumping, sliding, and near-miss combos to dominate the leaderboard.
🎨
Smooth Animation
Lumo runs smoothly with animated legs, tail wagging, and physics-based movement. Full particle effects on action.
🔊
Dynamic Soundtrack
Generated sound effects for jumps, coins, power-ups, and crashes. Toggle mute anytime with the button in-game.

How to Play

1
Jump & Slide, Jump over cacti, rocks, and pits. Slide under low obstacles.
2
Collect Coins, Grab coins above obstacles (+1 each). Find gems for +5 points.
3
Use Power-ups, Magnet pulls items, Shield blocks hits, Coin Rush doubles value.
4
Avoid Crashes, Hit an obstacle = game over. Score = distance + (coins ✨ 5).

Pro Tips

⏱️
Coyote Time: You can jump briefly after leaving ground for forgiving platforming
🎯
Near-Miss Combo: Obstacles passing behind you build a combo multiplier
💡
Double Jump: Press jump twice for extra height to clear tall obstacles
📊
Biome Colors: Watch for visual cues, each biome has different obstacle patterns

About This Game

Lumo Dash is a modern endless runner that combines classic arcade action with smooth physics and satisfying mechanics. Control Lumo the fox through procedurally spawning obstacles across three beautiful biomes. Master the timing of jumps and slides, collect power-ups strategically, and rack up the highest score. With delta-time physics, particle effects, dynamic sound, and challenging gameplay, it's the perfect game for quick sessions or extended play.

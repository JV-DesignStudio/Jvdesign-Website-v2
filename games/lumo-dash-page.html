How to Play

1
Start the Dash, Tap Start Dashing and Lumo the purple fox sets off through the enchanted forest.
2
Jump the Gaps, Tap or press Space/Up to leap over rocks, logs and gaps in the path.
3
Slide Under, Tap Slide or press Down to duck beneath low branches.
4
Grab Stardust, Sweep up glowing stardust for bonus points and push your best score higher.

Pro Tips

👀
Read the Trail: Watch the next obstacle early, gaps and branches come up fast
⬆
Time Your Jumps: Jump just before a rock or gap so Lumo lands clean on the far side
⬇
Slide Early: Start the slide as the branch arrives, not after you reach it
✦
Chase Stardust: Dashing through stardust adds points and keeps the glow bright

About This Game

Lumo's Dash is an endless runner starring Lumo the purple fox. Lumo dashes through the enchanted forest, jumping gaps and rocks and sliding under low branches while the world speeds up. Collect stardust for bonus points and push your distance record further every run. No install, no account, just open the browser and start dashing.

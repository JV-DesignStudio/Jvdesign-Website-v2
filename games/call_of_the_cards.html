? How to Play

A race to the crown 👑

Call of the Cards is a head-to-head competitive card game. Both players share a central Quest board whoever completes quests fastest and manages their gold wisest will claim 5 Victory Points and win.

On your turn, you spend Gold Cards from your hand to recruit Companions or acquire Artifacts. Spent Gold is discarded - draw carefully and spend wisely. Draw 1 at the start of each turn (2 with Amulet of Renewal).

Three Quests are always available in the centre. When you have enough combined Power from your Companions (plus Ring/Healer bonuses), you can complete a Quest to earn Victory Points.

Permanent Artifacts stay in play and give ongoing advantages. v1.2: Shadow Thief now forces a discard - there is real interaction.

🏆 Win Condition

Be the first player to accumulate 5 Victory Points by completing Quests. Assassin's Bounty is now 2 VP - mid-game quests matter.

📜 Quest-Driven Victory
Three Quests are always face-up. Complete one by meeting its Power requirement to earn VP. New quests replace completed ones immediately.
🛡️ Build Your Party
Recruit Companions like Captain (2g/3p), Elf Archer (3g/4p) or Healer (2g/2p +1 to all). Dwarven Smith draws 1 on recruit, Shadow Thief makes opponent discard.
💎 Collect Artifacts
Ring (4g +1 all), Shield (4g ignore 1 req), Sword (6g +1 VP) - costs rebalanced for physical play.
💰 Gold Management
Gold is fuel - you discard Gold cards to pay. You start with 5 cards, draw 1/turn. 48-card decks (12 Gold) - 108-card print set.
⚖️ Underdog's Resolve (v1.3)
If you're 2+ VP behind, draw +1 extra at start of turn (stacks with Amulet). Keeps comebacks alive. 108-card set: 46/deck +16 quests.

Version 1.2, Rebalanced for Physical
Rebalanced for print: Ring 3→4g, Sword 5→6g, Scout 1→2g, Siege 3→4p, Healer 1→2p (stacks with Ring), Shadow Thief 2→1p + discard interaction, Assassin 1→2 VP, Dwarven Smith draws on recruit. Gold now clearly discard-to-pay. Try it - feedback drives v1.3.
⚖️ Balance Update
October 25, 2025
Version 1.1, Gold Economy Rework
Addressed player feedback on the Gold economy. Gold is spent from hand and discarded - draw 1/turn keeps tension. Check the updated Playtest PDF!

Final Eight Artifacts Balanced
The Sword of Dawn, Amulet of Renewal, and six more Artifacts have been finalised and added to the official decklist. Power levels feel much better the Sword in particular went through four iterations before clicking.

First Public Playtest (v0.9)
The first public playtest edition went live! Thank you to everyone who jumped in and helped test the Quest-Driven Victory mechanic. Your feedback on game length and gold pacing directly shaped v1.1.

⚔️ How to Play

Storm the Fortress

Two rivals, one fortress. Play companion and trick cards, hold 8 Power at the start of a turn, then storm the gate before the Rival does. A full duel takes 3 to 5 minutes.

You start with 5 cards and earn 3 Gold every turn. Unspent Gold carries over, so you can save for a big play.

Companions stay on your side and add Power. Tricks are one-shot surprises: deal damage, draw cards, or boost your party for the turn. Artifacts stay in play and change the rules.

Hold 8 Power when your turn begins and the Storm button lights up. Press it to take the crown. The Rival is building the same party, so use tricks to slow them down.

🏆 Win Condition

Be the first to still hold 8 Power when your turn begins, then storm the Fortress. One fortress, one crown.

⚡ Power Race
Your party's total Power shows on the fortress. Fill six pips and the gate is yours.
💰 Gold Every Turn
Three Gold each turn, plus one from Boots of Haste. Save it for the turn that matters.
🗡️ Tricks Change the Race
Ambush, Fireball and Sneak Attack knock Power off the Rival's side. Rally and Fortify push yours over the line.
💍 Artifacts Bend the Rules
The Shield lowers the gate to 5 Power. The Ring and Sword add Power. The Amulet and Boots speed you up.
🤝 Play on Any Device
Tap or click to play. The hand scrolls sideways on a phone and the whole board fits a school Chromebook.

✦ Development Log

How the game got here 📖

Follow the evolution of Call of the Cards from initial prototype to the current playtest build.

🔮 Coming Next
Planned
Version 1.4, Art & 3-Player
Final art + 3-player variant after v1.3 physical tests.
⚖️ Balance Update
September 11, 2026
Version 1.3, 108 Cards + Catch-up
Print-ready 108 cards: 46/deck (10 Gold) +16 quests (Bandit Raid, Ancient Library, Goblin Market, Haunted Keep, Crown Courier, Starfall Summit). New Underdog's Resolve: +1 draw when 2+ VP behind (stacks with Amulet).
⚖️ Balance Update
September 11, 2026
Version 1.2, Rebalanced for Physical
Rebalanced for print: Ring 3→4g, Sword 5→6g, Scout 1→2g, Siege 3→4p, Healer 1→2p (stacks with Ring), Shadow Thief 2→1p + discard interaction, Assassin 1→2 VP, Dwarven Smith draws on recruit. Gold now clearly discard-to-pay. Try it - feedback drives v1.3.
⚖️ Balance Update
October 25, 2025
Version 1.1, Gold Economy Rework
Addressed player feedback on the Gold economy. Gold is spent from hand and discarded - draw 1/turn keeps tension. Check the updated Playtest PDF!
✅ Design Complete
October 10, 2025
Final Eight Artifacts Balanced
The Sword of Dawn, Amulet of Renewal, and six more Artifacts have been finalised and added to the official decklist. Power levels feel much better the Sword in particular went through four iterations before clicking.
🚀 Initial Launch
September 1, 2025
First Public Playtest (v0.9)
The first public playtest edition went live! Thank you to everyone who jumped in and helped test the Quest-Driven Victory mechanic. Your feedback on game length and gold pacing directly shaped v1.1.

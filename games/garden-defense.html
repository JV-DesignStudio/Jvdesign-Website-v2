Lumo's Garden Defense

The garden pests are marching in! Plant defenders, harvest sunlight and hold the line for five waves. Lumo has left a guard firefly on each row that clears it once when breached, so don't let them run out. Beat all five waves to save the garden!

Pip keeps your best score saved in your browser.

1. Start economyPlant a Sunflower early so sunlight keeps coming.
2. Hold lanesUse Sprouts for damage and Acorn Walls when a row is pressured.
3. Upgrade between wavesPick upgrades that match what went wrong last wave.

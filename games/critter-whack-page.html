Critter Whack

Read the ranger clue, then tap only the critters that fit. Echo needs a sharp-eyed ranger to sort each wave by habitat, movement or behaviour, and every wrong tap costs a life.

🔍 Ranger Clues
🎯 Tap & Click
📱 Mobile Ready
✨ Play Now
⏱️ Round: 45 seconds per wave
🐹 Critters: 7 species to sort
🌿 Sort by: Habitat · Movement · Behaviour

How to Play

1
Read the clue at the top of each wave, it tells you which critters fit
2
Tap matching critters as they pop out of the holes to score points
3
Leave the rest, a wrong critter or a hazard costs you a life
4
Build a streak of correct taps and beat your best score

Pro Tips

🔍
Read first: the clue names the group, such as flyers, swimmers, jumpers or night animals
📊
Streaks: correct taps in a row build your score faster
🎯
Careful taps: wrong critters and hazards both cost you a life
🔊
Sound: Toggle mute with the button in the top-right

About This Game

Critter Whack: Ranger Sort turns a quick reaction game into a sorting challenge. Each wave begins with a ranger clue, so players practise turning words into action and grouping critters by habitat, movement or behaviour. Echo set up the forest reserve and needs a ranger to send every critter to the right place.

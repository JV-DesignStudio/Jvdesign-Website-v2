Critter Whack

Tap the critters before they duck back down! Rack up points, avoid bombs, and beat your high score in this fast-paced whack-a-mole game.

⚡ Fast-Paced
🎯 Tap & Click
📱 Mobile Ready
▶ Play Now
⏱️ Duration: 30 seconds
🐹 Critters: 8 species
💣 Bombs: Avoid them!

How to Play

1
Click Start to begin your 30-second challenge
2
Tap critters as they pop out of holes to score points
3
Avoid bombs 💣 — each one costs you a life
4
Beat your score before time runs out!

Pro Tips

⚡
Speed up: Critters appear faster as time runs out
📊
Points: Each critter = 10 points, bombs = -1 life
🎯
Accuracy: Only tap active critters, misses don't hurt
🔊
Sound: Toggle mute with the button in the top-right

About This Game

Critter Whack is a modern take on the classic whack-a-mole arcade game. With vibrant visuals, satisfying sound effects, and challenging gameplay, it's perfect for quick gaming sessions on desktop or mobile. Can you beat the clock and rack up a high score?

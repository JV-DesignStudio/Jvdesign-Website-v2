✦ The World

Welcome to the Sweetest Place on Earth 🌈

The Candy Kingdom is a land of wonder lollipop trees, cake-soft ground, and rivers that flow with chocolate milk. Every creature here is made of sugar, every path is paved with something delicious.

But a sour shadow has fallen. The Naughty Sour Patch Kid has been at it again, twisting the sweet world sour with pranks and riddles. It's up to your brave candy heroes to solve his challenges and bring the fun and flavour back!

Perfect for families, classrooms, or any group wanting an easy first tabletop RPG experience no experience needed, just imagination and a six-sided die.

⚠️ Villain · Main Antagonist
😈 The Naughty Sour Patch Kid
Mischievous, sour, and absolutely full of pranks. He's made the royal moat sticky with caramel, created a maze of tangled candy cane trees, and left riddles only the cleverest heroes can solve. He doesn't want to hurt anyone he just wants chaos and giggles. Can your crew outsmart him?
🍋 Sour Magic 🌀 Trickster 🧩 Riddle Setter 😂 Loves Chaos
✦ How to Play

The Core Mechanic: Roll & React! 🎲

When a hero faces a challenge, they describe their plan and roll one six-sided die (d6). Simple, fast, and full of surprises!

4-6
✅ SUCCESS!
The idea works perfectly the path is clear, the puzzle is solved, the day is saved!
🎉
2-3
⚡ Partial Success
It works, but a small silly complication happens maybe they get a bit sticky, or drop something sweet!
😅
1
😬 Uh Oh!
The plan fails this time but that's okay! Time to think of a new idea and try again.
🎲
💪
Chewiness
Strength & toughness smashing, climbing, pushing
🧠
Sweet Smarts
Cleverness & magic riddles, recipes, planning
⚡
Fizzyness
Speed & agility leaping, sneaking, quick escapes
✦ The Candy Heroes

Choose your hero! 🍬

Six candy heroes with unique abilities each one specialises in a different stat and brings something special to the party.

🛡️
The Gummy Knight
💪 CHEWINESS Focused
A durable protector, tough as a gummy bear and just as bouncy. Built to take hits and push through any sticky situation.
✨ Special AbilitySuper Bounce! Leap over obstacles and push through messes with ease.
🌀
Peppermint Wizard
🧠 SWEET SMARTS Focused
Clever and cool-headed, this hero uses sticky magic to solve riddles and invent clever solutions on the spot.
✨ Special AbilitySticky Spell Create temporary bridges, ladders, or traps from peppermint magic.
🔪
Chocolate Rogue
⚡ FIZZYNESS Focused
Sneaky, swift, and smooth the Chocolate Rogue can slip through gaps and find secret paths nobody else notices.
✨ Special AbilityMelt into the Shadows Quick escapes and finding hidden passages.
✨
Caramel Cleric
🧠 SWEET SMARTS Focused
Warm, sticky, and wise the Caramel Cleric heals, supports, and helps the team stick together through any challenge.
✨ Special AbilityGolden Pour Restore a teammate's confidence after a failed roll.
⚡
Hard Candy Barbarian
💪 CHEWINESS Focused
Crystal-tough and fierce. The Hard Candy Barbarian smashes first and asks questions later great for blockades and big problems.
✨ Special AbilitySugar Rush Roll twice and take the higher result once per adventure.
🎶
Cotton Candy Bard
⚡ FIZZYNESS Focused
Fluffy, fun, and full of songs the Bard keeps spirits high and can use music to distract, charm, or inspire the whole party.
✨ Special AbilitySweet Melody Give another hero a +1 bonus on their next roll.
✦ Adventure 1

🍭 The Tangled Candy Cane Forest

Three challenges stand between your heroes and the Sour Patch Kid. Can you solve them all?

Challenge 1
🍯 The Sticky Floor
🐻 🍯 🐾
The forest path is completely covered in gooey, sticky caramel! A poor little Gummy Bear is already stuck in the middle. Heroes must find a way to cross without getting completely gummed up.
⚡ FizzynessLeap quickly from safe spot to safe spot.
🧠 Sweet SmartsFind a secret path of crunchy toffee pieces.
💪 ChewinessPush a bridge through or smash a path in the goo.
Challenge 2
💎 The Rock Candy Blockade
💎 🧱 💎
A solid, sparkling wall of rock candy completely blocks the path too high and too hard to climb! The heroes need a clever plan to get past this crystal obstacle.
💪 ChewinessSmash through a weak spot in the wall.
🧠 Sweet SmartsFigure out a magical chant or recipe to make the candy soft.
Challenge 3
🧩 The Flavour Riddle
🍬 🤔 🍬
"I have no mouth, but I can tell you a story. I'm not a book, but I have many words. What am I?"

(Hint: The answer is something found in the Candy Kingdom…)
Two candy canes stand at the end of the maze. One is sweet (the right path), one is sour (a trap). The only way to tell them apart is to answer the Sour Patch Kid's final riddle!
🧠 Sweet SmartsPuzzle out the riddle and choose wisely.

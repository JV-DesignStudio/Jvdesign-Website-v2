How to Play

1
Watch the block slide back and forth across the screen
2
Tap to drop the block onto your tower. Time it perfectly!
3
Stack perfectly to keep full width. Misses slice off the overhang.
4
Build as high as possible before hitting the ground or missing entirely

Pro Tips

🎯
Watch the edges: The block's position matters, aim for center alignment
⏱️
Timing is key: Practice the block's movement pattern to predict drops
📊
Width bonus: Perfect alignment gives you extra width, use it wisely
🎨
Color sequence: Blocks cycle through colors so you can anticipate patterns

About This Game

Stack Attack is a modern take on classic stacking arcade games. With its simple one-button mechanic, it's easy to learn but challenging to master. The core tension comes from timing, you must predict when to tap as the block slides, rewarding both quick reflexes and patience. The overhang mechanic adds strategic depth: should you take a risky perfect placement or play it safe? The colorful blocks, satisfying particle effects, and persistent scoring make it perfect for both casual and competitive play.

How to Play

1
Move the snake using arrow keys, WASD, swipe, or on-screen D-pad
2
Eat the apple 🍎 to grow longer and gain 10 points
3
Avoid walls and your own tail, hitting either ends the game
4
Survive as long as possible and rack up the highest score

Pro Tips

🎯
Plan ahead: Don't corner yourself, always leave room to maneuver
⏱️
Speed increases: Game gets faster as you eat more fruit, stay alert!
🎮
Best control: Swipe on mobile is most intuitive, arrow keys on desktop
📊
Scoring: Each fruit = 10 points. No time limit, play at your pace!

About This Game

Nibble Quest brings the timeless classic snake game to the modern web with polish and heart. Whether you're a longtime fan of the original game or discovering it for the first time, you'll find satisfying, addictive gameplay. The simple rules hide surprising depth, planning your path and managing your snake's growth creates engaging strategic moments. With beautiful visuals, responsive controls, and score persistence, it's the perfect game for quick sessions or extended play.

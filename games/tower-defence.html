How to Play

You start with a single 🔥 Hearth and nothing else.

Day - tap a building, then tap the ground to place it. 🌾 Farms give 🪙 gold + 🌾 food, 🏠 Houses give gold + 👥 population. 🗼 Towers shoot, 🧱 Walls block, 🚪 Gates funnel raiders to one weak point, ⚔️ Barracks trains soldiers each night.

It takes time to build. New buildings and upgrades start as 🚧 construction sites - villagers (👷) raise them while you wait, and more 🏠 Houses means faster building. Structures under construction don't work yet and are easy to knock down, so build before nightfall.

Tap any building you own to upgrade it (up to ★★★) or demolish it for half its cost. Tap a Barracks to switch its troops between ⚔️ melee and 🏹 archers.

Track your Milestones and fill in the Bestiary in the 📖 Chronicle. Try the 📅 Daily Challenge - the same seeded battle for everyone, scored by nights survived.

Night - raiders march on your Hearth, including 🏹 Archers that shoot from range and 🧨 Sappers that smash walls. Soldiers need food to muster and population to fill the ranks.

Drag anywhere to move your King. Tap 🚩 Rally to heal your troops and boost their damage for a few seconds.

Upgrade the Seat (the 🔥/🏛️/🏰 button) to grow your nation and advance through history.

Survive and reap a dawn tribute. Every night is harder - how long can you hold?

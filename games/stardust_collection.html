✦ How to Play

Collect. Dodge. Shine.

Move your star across the field, collect everything that glitters, and dodge anything that isn't stardust.

⬅️➡️
Move Your Star
Arrow keys or A/D on keyboard. Drag on mobile. Your little star follows wherever you go.
✨
Collect Stardust
Stardust, Moon Gems, Crystals and Nebula Diamonds fall from above — each worth more points than the last.
🔗
Build Combos
Chain collections without missing to multiply your score. The longer the streak, the bigger the multiplier.
☄️
Dodge Hazards
Shadow Comets, Asteroids, Volatile Fragments and Black Holes will end your run. Each hit costs a life.
🛡️
Power-Ups
Grab the Shield to become temporarily invincible, or Speed to dart across the field faster.
🏆
Beat Your Best
Your high score is saved locally. Three lives per run — how high can you get before the cosmos claims you?

How to Play

Pilot your star, collect what shines, dodge what doesn't.

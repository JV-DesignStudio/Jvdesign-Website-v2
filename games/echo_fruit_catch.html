✦ How to Play

Super simple even tiny hands can play!

🍎
Catch the Fruit!
Move Echo left and right to catch yummy falling fruit. Each one is worth points!
🪨
Dodge the Rocks!
Watch out, rocks fall too! Catch a rock and Echo loses a heart. Three hits and game over!
⭐
Grab Stars!
Rare golden stars fall sometimes. Catch them for bonus points they're worth triple!
🚀
Speed Goes Up!
Every 10 fruit caught, fruit falls faster. How long can you keep going?

Cozy Creatures:
The Ultimate Snuggle

The rain is falling, the house is warm, and the perfect cozy afternoon is within reach but the Sunshine Break is creeping closer. Gather your Cozy Crew and beat the weather before it clears!

👥 1-4 Players ⏱ 30-45 min 🤝 Cooperative 🌧️ Rainy Day Vibes
📖 Read the Rules → 🃏 See the Cards
Players1-4
Rain Deck15 cards
Sunshine Limit3 = Loss
AP per Turn3 AP
Inventory2 cards
Win bySnuggle Goal
✦ How to Play

The storm is the clock ⏱

Cozy Creatures is a fully cooperative game everyone wins together or loses together. Draw from the Rain Deck each round; if 3 Sunshine Break cards ever appear face-up, the team loses. Complete your Snuggle Goal to win.

Each player takes turns spending their 3 Action Points (AP) to move around the house, search rooms for ingredients, carry items back to the Living Room, and assemble Cozy Item Tokens.

The Rain Deck acts as your timer each round you draw from it. Mostly you'll draw Steady Rain (no effect), but the three Sunshine Break cards lurk within. Once three are face-up, the moment of cosiness has passed and the team loses.

Choose a Snuggle Goal before starting to set your difficulty. A Simple Snuggle is a great first game; The Ultimate Snuggle is peak challenge.

✅ Win Condition
The team successfully assembles all required Cozy Item Tokens listed on the chosen Snuggle Goal card in the Living Room before the rain ends.
❌ Loss Conditions
  • 3 Sunshine Break cards are face-up at any point
  • The Rain Deck is fully depleted before the goal is complete
🎲 Setup
Shuffle each Search Deck separately. Shuffle the Rain Deck and place it face-down. Each player chooses a character and takes their card. Draw a Snuggle Goal. All players start in the Living Room.
✦ Actions

What you can do on your turn

Every player has 3 Action Points (AP) per turn. Spend them on any combination of the actions below.

1 AP
Move
Move 1 space to an adjacent room. Costs 1 AP per space.
1 AP
Search
Draw 1 card from the Search Deck in your current room and add it to your hand (up to inventory limit).
1 AP
Share
Give 1 card from your hand to a teammate in the same room. (Turtle: 0 AP)
3 AP
Assemble
Spend 3 AP in the Living Room to convert the required ingredient cards into a Cozy Item Token.
1 AP
Quick Kettle
Fox only. Convert a Hot Cocoa or Marshmallow card into a Hot Drink token immediately.
1 AP
Focused Read
Witch-Hat Cat only. Spend 1 AP to swap locations with any other player.
✦ The Card Decks

Four rooms, four decks 🃏

Each room has its own Search Deck. Good cards bring you closer to your Snuggle Goal hazard cards cost you time or AP. The Rain Deck is always the timer.

Meet the Crew ✶

Five adventurers from a world where kindness is the greatest magic, each one ready for their next story.

Lumo

The Purple Fox

Kind and curious, Lumo loves exploring and discovering the magic hidden in everyday things.

Pip

The Little Turtle

Gentle and kind-hearted, small in size, but Pip's courage is as big as the wide forest.

Stardust

The Dream Spirit

Made from dreams and tiny pieces of stars, wherever someone needs hope, Stardust appears.

Ember

The Pink Tiger

Brave, curious, and big-hearted, a flame of adventure and protector of those they care about.

Echo

The Axolotl Explorer

A tiny adventurer in a dark green cloak, clutching an aged brass compass, always ready for the next great discovery.

Barnaby

The Arctic Fox

Barnaby the arctic fox is busy sorting his socks, until he remembers how to play. Snow-white fur, a long nose and a snow-tipped tail, always separate from Lumo the purple fox.

Five characters, one world. Lumo, Pip, Stardust, Ember, and Echo appear across JVDesignStudio's books, games, and courses, each one bringing their own kind of magic.

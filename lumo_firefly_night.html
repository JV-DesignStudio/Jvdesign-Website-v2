Shoot coloured fireflies into the hex grid, match three or more, and clear the board before they reach Lumo. Special bubbles, power-ups, and chain drops — free to play in your browser.

✦ How to Play

Aim. Shoot. Match. Clear.

Lumo stands at the bottom holding his aim arrow. You have a current bubble and a preview of the next one. Clear the whole board to advance.

🎯
Aim & Shoot
Move your cursor to aim, click to fire a firefly into the hex grid above.
✨
Match 3+
Match 3 or more fireflies of the same colour to pop them. Floating clusters drop automatically.
⚡
Special Bubbles
Rainbow, Bomb, Star, Freeze and Scatter bubbles change everything. Use them wisely.
🏆
Clear the Board
Pop every firefly to complete the level. Miss too many shots and new rows appear as a penalty.
💥
Power-Ups
Collect power-ups for Multi-shot, Bomb, Slow-mo, Ghost and Freeze to turn the tide.
📈
Level Up
Each level adds more rows, more colours, and trickier special bubbles. How far can you go?

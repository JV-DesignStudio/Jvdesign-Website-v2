Make a character

Draw a sprite, animate it, then share a character card.

  1. 1 Draw your sprite Pixel Studio. Start with a simple 8x8 or 16x16 character.
  2. 2 Animate it Sprite Animator. Add a few frames and set the timing.
  3. 3 Share the card Trading Card Designer. Drop in your art and export a PNG. Export
🕹️

Make a game

Draw a hero, build a level, then share a playable game.

  1. 1 Make the hero Pixel Studio. Draw one character sprite for your game.
  2. 2 Build the world World Builder. Lay out a small level with platforms.
  3. 3 Ship and share it Arcade Game Maker. Load your art and level, then share a playable link. Share
🎵

Make music and sound

Start a loop, add a sound effect, then hear it in a game.

  1. 1 Make a loop Audio Studio. Use Music mode for a short backing loop.
  2. 2 Add a sound effect Audio Studio. Switch to SFX and make a jump or coin sound.
  3. 3 Put it in a game Arcade Game Maker. Load your sounds and share the build. Share

Get unstuck

Find the fix, copy working code, then plan your next move.

  1. 1 Find the bug Error Guide. Look up the error in plain language.
  2. 2 Grab a working snippet Code Snippet Generator. Copy a starter for the pattern you need.
  3. 3 Plan the fix Game Planner. Turn the fix into quests and export the plan. Export

Design Like a Maker
Episode 12: Design Your World

Anyone can place tiles. This episode is about why you place them where you do: giving a place a purpose, guiding the player, and making it feel finished. It works in the Mod Studio and in any game you build.

Start With a Purpose
What is this place for?
Active
🎯
Goal for this step

Decide the purpose and mood before you paint a single tile.

  • 1Write one sentence: "This place is for ___ and should feel ___."
  • 2For example: "a quiet reading garden that feels calm".
  • 3Your purpose decides the tiles: a calm place uses water, soft paths and few bright objects.
  • 4Paint the ground first on the Back layer to match that mood.

Guide the Eye
Paths and one focal point
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Use a path to lead the player and one focal point to look at.

  • 1Lay a path from the entrance to the heart of the place. The path is a quiet instruction: "come this way".
  • 2Add one focal point: a big tree, a pond, a building. The eye should land somewhere.
  • 3Don't give the eye ten things to look at. One clear focal point beats five busy ones.
  • 4Use the Studio's Rect and Fill tools to lay paths fast, then decorate.

Keep the Flow
Never trap the player
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Check that every space connects and nothing traps the player.

  • 1Flow is how easy it is to move around. A good place has clear routes, not dead ends.
  • 2Play-test and try to get stuck. If you can, fix it with the tile rules or by moving a wall.
  • 3Leave a gap of at least one tile between solid objects so the player can slip through.
  • 4Connect your area to the rest of the map with a path or a warp, so it is part of the world.

Balance and Detail
Space to breathe, then a signature touch
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Leave open space, then add details that feel placed, not scattered.

  • 1Balance: leave some open ground. Empty space is not wasted, it makes the details stand out.
  • 2Group details in odd numbers (3 flowers, not 2 or 4). It looks more natural.
  • 3Add one signature detail that says "I made this": a special statue, a colour, a little camp.
  • 4Use layer toggles in the Studio to check each layer on its own, so nothing is buried by mistake.

Test Like a Player
Walk it and fix the boring bits
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Play your world and fix what does not work.

  • 1Play your world. Walk the path you made. Did it lead somewhere worth going?
  • 2Try to get lost or stuck. A real player will do exactly that.
  • 3Find one boring or empty part and add a detail or a reason to visit.
  • 4Fix one thing, then play again. Design is testing and improving, not one perfect pass.

The Design Checklist
Finish and claim the badge
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Run the checklist on your world, then finish the series.

  • 1Purpose: can you say what this place is for in one sentence?
  • 2Guide: is there a path, and one clear thing to look at?
  • 3Flow: can the player move around without getting stuck?
  • 4Balance & detail: open space, grouped details, one signature touch?
  • 5If all four are yes, you have designed a world, not just filled a grid. Finish every episode for the Farm Architect badge.

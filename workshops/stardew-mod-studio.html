Stardew Mod Studio
Build a world in your browser

Practise the whole Stardew modding job without the game: paint a map with real layers and tile rules, walk it to test, set a doorway warp, write your manifest and content files, debug a SMAPI log, then download your mod. It works on a locked-down school computer.

🛠️ Open the Studio ← Back to the Workshop
🐯
Ember says: the real game needs an install, but the skills do not. Everything here is the same as Tiled and Content Patcher, just friendlier. Your work saves automatically, so you can close the tab and come back.

How this maps to Tiled

In Stardew a doorway is a Warp value on the map: fromX fromY toArea toX toY. Set the two doorways below, then test the walk. A doorway with no way back strands the player.

Map: Farm

Map: Cabin

Test it

The Farm door is at tile 8,4. Its value 8 4 Cabin 2 4 means: step on 8,4 and arrive on Cabin at 2,4. The Cabin door does the trip back.

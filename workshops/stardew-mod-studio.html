Echo's recovery check

Stuck? Try this

These are the three things that most often stop a first Stardew world. Check the symptom first, then try the fix.

Painting does nothing

What you'll see: clicking the map leaves it unchanged.

Fix: Pick a tile in the toolbar, check the layer you are painting on, then click the map.

The walk test will not move

What you'll see: the arrow pad does not appear or move you.

Fix: Press Walk test, then use the arrow pad. Press it again to close the test.

The export will not open

What you'll see: the zip does not load in Stardew Valley.

Fix: Unzip the download, check the file list, then put the folder in your SMAPI mods folder.

Still stuck? Reread the step, open the Stardew cheat sheet, or ask an adult to sit with you.

How this maps to Tiled

In Stardew a doorway is a Warp value on the map: fromX fromY toArea toX toY. Set the two doorways below, then test the walk. A doorway with no way back strands the player.

The Farm door is at tile 8,4. Its value 8 4 Cabin 2 4 means: step on 8,4 and arrive on Cabin at 2,4. The Cabin door does the trip back.

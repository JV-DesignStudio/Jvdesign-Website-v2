Stardew Mod Studio
Build a world in your browser

A real little map engine for the Stardew World Workshop: paint with layers and tile rules, fill and undo, take on challenges, walk-test your world, set a doorway warp, write your files, debug a SMAPI log, save or share your world, then download your mod. It runs on a locked-down school computer.

How this maps to Tiled

In Stardew a doorway is a Warp value on the map: fromX fromY toArea toX toY. Set the two doorways below, then test the walk. A doorway with no way back strands the player.

Map: Farm

Map: Cabin

Test it

The Farm door is at tile 8,4. Its value 8 4 Cabin 2 4 means: step on 8,4 and arrive on Cabin at 2,4. The Cabin door does the trip back.

Download your mod

        
This bundles everything you made into a real mod folder and downloads it as a zip: manifest.json, content.json, your map as a .tmx, and a README. Drop the unzipped folder into Stardew Valley / Mods.

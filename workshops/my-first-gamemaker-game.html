Questions & Answers

Do I need to know GML before starting?
No. We explain every line of GML (GameMaker Language) as we write it. GML is a beginner-friendly scripting language it reads almost like plain English and is much simpler than C# or C++.
Is GameMaker really free?
The free tier of GameMaker lets you build and export games to Windows at no cost. A paid subscription unlocks exports to Mac, iOS, Android and consoles but for learning, free is more than enough.
How is GameMaker different from Unity or Unreal?
GameMaker is purpose-built for 2D games and is much simpler to get started with. It runs on almost any computer, starts up instantly and gets you to a playable game faster than either Unity or Unreal. Great for beginners who want to see results quickly.
What age is it suitable for?
Episode 1 works well from age 9 with adult support. Episodes 2 and 3 involve more GML and suit ages 10–11+ who are comfortable copying and editing code. GameMaker is one of the friendliest engines for young learners.
Can I publish my GameMaker game?
Yes! Free tier exports run on Windows. You can upload a Windows executable to itch.io so friends can download and play. A paid subscription lets you export to web (HTML5) and mobile too.
Is it really free?
All 3 episodes, forever. No ads, no paywall, no sign-up required here. GameMaker's free tier and YoYo Games account are both free to create.

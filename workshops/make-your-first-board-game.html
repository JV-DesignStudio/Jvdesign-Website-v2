Six small steps

1

Pick a board

Open Tabletop Studio and choose Snakes and Ladders. Lumo says: a ready board is a good place to start.

2

Change one tile

Use the Tile tool on a square. Give it a number, a name or a rule like “move forward 3”.

3

Add one card

Open Cards. Make a card called “Lucky Day” that says “Roll again”. It joins the deck.

4

Start a game

Tap Play, then Set up player pawns. The pawns wait at tile 1.

5

Play a turn

Press Roll for Player 1. Pick a pawn if asked, then watch it climb or slide.

6

Improve one thing

Echo asks: what was confusing? Change one tile, one rule or one card, then play again.

Your checklist

  • I picked a board.
  • I changed one tile or one rule.
  • I added one card to a deck.
  • I rolled, chose a pawn and moved it.
  • I saved the board or copied a share code.
  • I wrote one thing I will change next time.

Stuck? Try this

No dice movement?

Make sure you are in Play mode and a route exists. Use Serpentine 1..N in the Route panel to number the whole board in one tap.

Pawns in the wrong place?

Tap Set up player pawns to put them back at tile 1. Then roll again.

Not sure what to change?

Make one snake a ladder, or add a tile that sends a player back to the start. Small changes are the whole point.

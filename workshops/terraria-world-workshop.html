Terraria World Workshop
Build Your Own World

Start with a world you know, then carve it, fill it and grow it into somewhere nobody has seen before. You will edit real Terraria worlds in the free TEdit editor, then save and share a world of your own.

🛡️
Two ways to learn this: the full series needs the paid Terraria game plus free mod tools (tModLoader and TEdit), so it is a home-computer job best with a grown-up. But you can start right now, with no install, in the browser studio, on any computer including a school one.
🐯
Ember says: we build in small steps, and each one ends with something you can actually see in the world. No giant leaps. By the last episode you will have your own world that you can load, save and share.
🛠️ Open the Terraria World Studio A browser playground for this series: paint and dig tiles, build a biome, add chests, walk and dig to test, plan your world files, debug a tModLoader log and download your world plan. No install, works on a school computer. Start building →
⏱️ Your first 15 minutes (no install)
  1. Open the World Studio.
  2. Paint some dirt, stone, grass and a pond on the map, then add a house.
  3. Press Start walk test and try to walk into the stone. It should block you.
  4. On the Download your world tab, download your world plan. That is a real plan you can build from.

Then, when you have the game and a grown-up to help with the install, start Episode 1 and take it into Terraria for real.

The 12 Episodes

Follow them in order. Each episode is about 45 minutes and adds one new skill.

Episode 1 · Beginner
Get Set Up 🌳
Install tModLoader and TEdit, find your Worlds folder and read the load log.
Episode 2 · Beginner
Inside a World File 🗺️
Tour the Worlds folder and learn what a .wld file really holds: tiles, walls, chests and metadata.
Episode 3 · Beginner
Your First Change ⛏️
Copy a world, open it in TEdit, change one tile, save it and load it back in the game.
Episode 4 · Builder
Walls & Layers 🧱
Foreground and background walls, and why a wall stops enemies spawning.
Episode 5 · Builder
Biomes & Rules ❄️
What makes a biome a biome, then place your own Snow and Desert patch.
Episode 6 · Builder
Build & Decorate 🏠
A house, platforms, furniture, chests and lighting to turn a plot into a place.
Episode 7 · Builder
Tunnels & Traversal 🪜
Dig a safe tunnel, add ropes and platforms so the player can travel.
Episode 8 · Advanced
A Brand-New Area 🏟️
Carve a brand-new arena from empty rock and connect it with a tunnel.
Episode 9 · Advanced
Package & Share Your World 📦
Save your .wld, name it well and share it with a friend.
Episode 10 · Advanced
Fix It 🔧
Read the log, fix version and save mistakes, and back up your world.
Episode 11 · Capstone
Play & Share 🌳
Run around your world with real collision, save it and share a world page.
Episode 12 · Design
Design Your World 🎨
Think like a level designer: purpose, guide the eye, flow, balance and detail.

Try it now: Sketch your world

Plan a layout in your browser before you open TEdit. Pick a tile, click or drag on the grid to paint, then download your plan. This bit needs no install and no game, so you can start right now.

Tip: paint the ground first, then stone, then trees and chests on top. "Save plan" gives you a file you can open again or build from in Episode 3.

🧰 What you need

A computer (Windows, Mac or Linux), the paid Terraria game, and a grown-up to help with the first install.

🌳 The badge

Finish all 12 episodes to earn the World Builder badge on your progress page.

📦 Starter files

Every episode is in the starter pack. All the steps also have a Copy button.

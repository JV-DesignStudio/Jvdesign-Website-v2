What a Biome Really Is
A region counted from the blocks around it
Active
🎯
Goal for this step

Understand a biome as a count of the blocks near your player.

  • 1A biome is a region of the world with its own look, its own music and its own enemies.
  • 2The game does not draw biomes on a map. It counts the blocks around your player.
  • 3Pile up a lot of sand in one area and the game decides you are in a Desert.
  • 4So a biome is really a rule: enough of the right blocks, close enough together.

Block Requirements
Sand makes Desert, snow makes Snow
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Learn which blocks make which biome.

  • 1Each biome needs a lot of its own blocks in one area before it appears.
  • 2Lots of sand makes a Desert. Snow and ice make a Snow biome.
  • 3Jungle grass and mud make a Jungle. Corrupt and crimson blocks grow their own biomes.
  • 4The blocks have to be close together. A few tiles dotted far apart will not count.

Place a Biome Patch
Paint the blocks in TEdit
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Paint a patch of biome blocks big enough to count.

  • 1In TEdit, pick the block you need, like snow, ice or sand.
  • 2Paint a patch of it along the surface, wide and thick enough to count.
  • 3One or two tiles is not a patch. Aim for about a room-sized block of it.
  • 4Keep the patch together, not dotted around, so the count really adds up.

Biome Size and Boundaries
A few tiles is not enough
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Learn how size and edges decide whether a biome shows up.

  • 1A biome needs enough blocks to cross a hidden threshold. A few tiles will not do it.
  • 2The bigger the area, the more reliably the biome triggers.
  • 3Biomes have soft edges. The change happens gradually as you walk in.
  • 4If two patches sit close together they can blend, so leave room between different biomes.

Fix a Biome That Will Not Trigger
Check the blocks and the count
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Fix a biome that stays stubbornly plain.

  • 1If nothing changes, check you painted the right block. The wrong block does nothing.
  • 2Make sure the blocks are dense enough and close together, not scattered.
  • 3Check the blocks are somewhere that counts, not buried far out of reach.
  • 4Save, reload the world, then walk in again to test. Repeat until it works.

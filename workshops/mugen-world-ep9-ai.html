Goal for this step

Decide behaviour by how far the enemy is.

  • 1Find the distance between the fighters each moment.
  • 2Far: walk forward. Mid: approach or poke. Close: attack or block.
  • 3Three ranges make an AI that reads well.

Goal for this step

Make the AI block sometimes.

  • 1An AI that only attacks is easy to beat.

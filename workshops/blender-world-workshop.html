🔗 How the two work together

You use our browser 3D engine and Blender as one workflow, so you can start making a model with nothing installed and finish it like a pro:

  1. Block out in our engine. Open BuildLab and place, paint and resize blocks to get the shape right. No install, works on a school computer.
  2. Export a Blender file. In BuildLab choose Blender (.glb / .obj) and download your model.
  3. Import it in Blender. Open Blender and use File > Import > glTF 2.0 (or Wavefront .obj). Your blocks arrive as a real 3D model.
  4. Follow the episodes. Use each episode to add detail, rig it, animate it, add materials, light it and render.

The 8 Episodes

Follow them in order. Each episode adds one new skill and ends with something you can see or render.

Episode 1 · Beginner
Hello 3D! 🧊
Install Blender, learn to fly the camera, and light your very first cube scene.
Episode 2 · Beginner
Character Basics 🧑
Build a simple character shape out of 3D objects and find your way around the mesh.
Episode 3 · Improver
Rigging & Bones 🦴
Give your model a skeleton so it can be posed and moved.
Episode 4 · Improver
Character Animation 🎬
Make it move: keyframes, timing and a short looping action.
Episode 5 · Improver
Materials & Textures 🎨
Colour and texture your model so it looks like a real thing.
Episode 6 · Advanced
Lighting & Effects 💡
Set the mood with lights, shadows and a bit of atmosphere.
Episode 7 · Advanced
Complete Scene 🌍
Bring it all together and render a finished picture or clip.
Episode 8 · Capstone
Blender Beginners Course 🐵
The full guided course that ties every skill together, start to finish.

🧰 What you need

A computer, the free Blender app (from blender.org), and a mouse with a scroll wheel if you can. Blender runs on modest computers, and the blockout sketch above works in any browser.

🎨 The badge

Finish all 8 episodes to earn the 3D Artist Pro badge on your progress page.

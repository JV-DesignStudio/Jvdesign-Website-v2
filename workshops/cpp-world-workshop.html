🔗 How the two work together

You use our browser studio and real C++ as one workflow, so you can start building with nothing installed and compile it when the tools are ready:

  1. Lay out a level in our studio. Open the C++ Mod Studio and place a player, platforms, bricks and a goal. No install.
  2. Download the project. The studio writes a whole main.cpp, a CMakeLists.txt and a README.
  3. Install your tools once. Get a C++ compiler and SFML, then build with cmake -B build and cmake --build build.
  4. Follow the episodes. Use each episode to learn a new idea, from Pong and Snake all the way to a Tower Defence game.

The 7 Episodes

Follow them in order. Each episode adds one new idea and ends with a game you can play.

Episode 1 · Beginner
Pong 🏓
Your first C++ game: a window, a paddle, a ball and two players.
Episode 2 · Beginner

Grow a snake, eat the food and do not bite your own tail.
Episode 3 · Beginner
Breakout 🧱
A paddle, a ball and a wall of bricks: the classic arcade game.
Episode 4 · Improver
Simple Platformer 🎮
Gravity, jumping and platforms that really hold you up.
Episode 5 · Improver
Platformer Part 2 🏃

Episode 6 · Advanced
Tower Defence Part 1 🏰
Build a path, place towers and stop the enemies getting through.
Episode 7 · Capstone
Tower Defence Part 2 👑
Upgrades, waves and polish to finish your biggest C++ game.

🧰 What you need

A computer, a C++ compiler (such as the free MinGW or Visual Studio Build Tools) and the free SFML library. The C++ Mod Studio above works in any browser.

What is My First Series?

My First Series is a collection of complete, step-by-step game-building pathways. Each series guides you and your child through learning a real game engine from the very first click to building and sharing a finished game. Every series is structured the same way: a series of episodes, each teaching one concept, building on the last.

🎮

Hands-On Learning

Every episode includes a live interactive builder or step-by-step guide. You and your child build real, playable games not just watch videos.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Parent & Child Together

Designed for learning together. A parent guides, a child explores. One teaches, the other discovers. Both build something real.

🚀

No Experience Needed

Zero coding knowledge required to start. Each series begins at the absolute beginning. All you need is curiosity and 30 minutes together.

📚

Learn Real Concepts

Not just for fun. You'll learn real game development concepts: variables, loops, collision detection, AI, animation, and more.

🎯

Complete Series

Each series is self-contained. 3-7 episodes depending on the engine. By the last episode, you'll have a finished, playable game.

💯

100% Free

Every series is completely free. No accounts, no logins, no surprise paywalls. Just open, learn, and build together.

Which Series Should We Pick?

It depends on what excites you. Prefer visual programming? Start with Scratch. Love classic game engines? Try Godot. Want to build with friends online? Roblox is your answer. Or pick the engine that's most fun to you that's always the right choice.

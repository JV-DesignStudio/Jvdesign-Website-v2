3D Co-op Edition: The Base & Co-op Mechanics
Age 8+ with grown-up ⏱ 2 to 3 hours Godot 4 · Free!

👶 FOR THE LITTLE CODER

Instead of flat paper on a desk, we're walking around a real 3D world! You can walk up to the gate, look at survivors face-to-face, and team up with your grown-up to run the base.

👨‍👩‍👧 FOR THE GROWN-UP

Moving from 2D UI to 3D requires CharacterBody3D nodes and Godot's built-in High-Level Multiplayer (ENetMultiplayerPeer) so players can connect and share the same checkpoint lobby.

🏆 BASE SECURED!

You built a working 3D co-op checkpoint! Part 2 adds the 10-day economy, piggy banks, rent, escalating rules, and a win condition.

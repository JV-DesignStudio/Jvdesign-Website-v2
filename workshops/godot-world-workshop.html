Make Your First Godot Game

Godot is a free, friendly game engine. Design a scene right now in your browser, download it as a real Godot project, then follow the episodes to build a platformer, a racing game and a 3D level. By the last episode you will have games you made yourself.

🔗 How the two work together

You use our browser studio and Godot as one workflow, so you can start building with nothing installed and finish it in the real engine:

  1. Lay out a scene in our studio. Open the Godot Mod Studio and place a player, platforms, coins and a goal on the grid. No install.
  2. Download the project. The studio writes a project.godot, a Main.tscn scene and your GDScript, all in one zip.
  3. Open it in Godot. Install the free Godot (godotengine.org), choose Import, and pick project.godot, then press F5 to run.
  4. Follow the episodes. Use each episode to turn the starting scene into a full platformer, racer or 3D level.

The 7 Episodes

Follow them in order. Each episode adds one new skill and ends with something you can play.

Episode 1 · Beginner
Your First Platformer 🎮
Start from an empty project and build a playable 2D platformer, step by step.
Episode 2 · Beginner
GDScript Essentials 📜
Learn the language Godot speaks, so the code in every other episode makes sense.
Episode 3 · Beginner
Racing Game 🏎️
Build a top-down racing game with a car you can drive around a track.
Episode 4 · Improver
Racing Part 2: Loops & Explosions 💥
Add laps, health and effects to make your racer feel like a real game.
Episode 5 · Improver
Who Goes There? Part 1 🚪
Move into 3D and build a co-op checkpoint you can walk around in.
Episode 6 · Advanced
Who Goes There? Part 2 🏆
The 10-day challenge: polish your 3D level and test it with a friend.
Episode 7 · Capstone
Godot 4 Starter Templates 🧩
Ready-made project kits to jump-start your own game from what you have learned.

🧰 What you need

A computer, the free Godot app (from godotengine.org), and a mouse if you can. Godot runs on modest computers, and the Mod Studio above works in any browser.

🐙 The badge

Finish all 7 episodes to earn the Godot Game Dev Pro badge on your progress page.

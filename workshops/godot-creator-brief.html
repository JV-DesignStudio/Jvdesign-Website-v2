The brief: The Night Market

Make a short top-down level set in a night market. A player starts at one end, collects three lanterns and reaches the exit. The level must teach the player one thing without words.

  • 1A start and a finish the player can find.
  • 2Three lanterns to collect.
  • 3One hazard or obstacle that can be seen before it is too late.
  • 4One original feature you choose. It must be visible in the level.
  • 5It should take a player under two minutes to finish.

Write a five-line plan that names the route, the hazard and your original feature.

Do not open Godot yet. A Creator plans before building. Draw the route on paper or type it below.

Echo's recovery check

Stuck? Try this

These are the three things that most often stop a Creator first pass. Check the symptom first, then try the fix.

The level is too big to finish

What you'll see: You have been building for a long time and there is still no exit.

Fix: Cut the level to one room. Place the start, three lanterns and the exit. Delete anything that is not needed for those five things.

The browser canvas will not run

What you'll see: The preview pane stays blank or says it cannot load.

Fix: Open the platformer canvas on its own page, or use the Godot Lab. If the school network blocks the Lab, build in the canvas and finish at home in Godot.

You cannot see your original feature

What you'll see: The level plays, but the thing you added is not noticeable.

Fix: Make it bigger, put it on the main route, or give it a sound or colour the rest of the level does not use. If a player walks past it, it is not part of the level yet.

Still stuck? Reread the brief, open the Godot cheat sheet, or ask an adult to play and tell you where they stopped.

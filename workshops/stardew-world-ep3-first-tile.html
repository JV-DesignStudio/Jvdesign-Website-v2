Copy the Farm Map
Make your own copy before changing anything
Active
🎯
Goal for this step

Get a copy of the farm map into your mod's assets folder.

  • 1In your Stardew Valley folder, open Content / Maps and find the farm map, Farm.tmx.
  • 2Copy Farm.tmx and paste it into your mod at assets/maps/Farm.tmx.
  • 3The map uses tilesheet images. Copy those image files into the same assets/maps folder so the paths still match.
  • 4You now have your own copy. The game's original is still safe.

Open It in Tiled
Say hello to the map editor
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Open your copied map in Tiled and orient yourself.

  • 1Open Tiled, then File > Open and choose your assets/maps/Farm.tmx.
  • 2On the right you see the Layers panel (Back, Buildings, Front and more). On the bottom left are the Tilesets.
  • 3In the middle is the map. Click the stamp brush icon to paint tiles.
  • 4Click a tile in the Tileset panel to select it. That is the tile you will paint.

Paint One Tile
Change a bit of grass into something new
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Make a small, obvious change to the map.

  • 1Select the Back layer so you paint on the ground layer.
  • 2Pick a grass or path tile in the Tileset panel.
  • 3Click on the map to paint your tile over a small patch. Start with just a few tiles, easy to spot.
  • 4Made a mistake? Ctrl+Z undoes it. Nothing here is permanent until you save.
💡
Paint a little smiley or your initial in the grass. It is easy to spot when you load the game, and it feels great when it shows up.

Save the Map
Keep the change on disk
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Save your edited map back to your mod.

  • 1Press Ctrl+S (or File > Save) and save as assets/maps/Farm.tmx.
  • 2Tiled saves in the same .tmx format Stardew uses, so no conversion is needed.
  • 3If Tiled asks about image paths, keep them in the same folder so the map can find its tilesheets.
  • 4The change is saved but the game does not know about it yet. Step 5 fixes that.

Tell the Game to Load It
One Load action in content.json
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Point Content Patcher at your map.

  • 1Open content.json and add a Load change.
  • 2Target is the game's map, Maps/Farm. FromFile is your copy, assets/maps/Farm.tmx.
  • 3Save content.json.
  • 4Now the game will use your map instead of its own, without overwriting anything.
content.json
{
  "Format": "2.9.0",
  "LogName": "My First Farm",
  "Changes": [
    {
      "Action": "Load",
      "Target": "Maps/Farm",
      "FromFile": "assets/maps/Farm.tmx"
    }
  ]
}

See It in the Game
Launch, walk over, and celebrate
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Load a save and find your change on the farm.

  • 1Launch Stardew through SMAPI and load any farm save.
  • 2Walk to the spot you painted. Your new tiles should be there.
  • 3Not showing? Check the console for red lines, and check your FromFile path matches the real file name exactly.
  • 4It is there? You just modded Stardew Valley. The rest of the series is bigger versions of this.

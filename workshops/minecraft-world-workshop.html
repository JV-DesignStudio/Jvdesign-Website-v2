No install needed: every episode here builds a datapack, which is just a folder of text files. That means no mod loader, no admin rights and no powerful PC, so it works at home and on a school computer. You do need Minecraft Java Edition to test in-game, but you can start in the browser Studio right now.
🔥
Ember says: we build in small steps, and each one ends with something you can actually see in the game. A datapack is the friendliest mod of all: it is just a folder of text files, and Minecraft reads it for you.
🛠️ Open the Minecraft Mod Studio A browser playground for this series: lay out a crafting recipe on a 3x3 bench, add a function of commands, fill in pack.mcmeta and your recipe file, debug a broken datapack log, then download a real datapack zip. No install, works on a school computer. Start building →
⏱️ Your first 15 minutes (no install)
  1. Open the Mod Studio.
  2. On the Crafting bench, put two diamonds above one stick to make a sword shape.
  3. On the Your files tab, give your datapack a name and pick your Minecraft version.
  4. On the Download your datapack tab, download the zip. That is a real datapack.

Drop the unzipped folder into saves/<your world>/datapacks/ and type /reload in-game. Your recipe shows up at any crafting table.

The 8 Episodes

Follow them in order. Each episode is about 45 minutes and adds one new skill.

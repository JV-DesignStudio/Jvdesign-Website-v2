Minecraft Mod Studio
A free browser playground for the Minecraft World Workshop. Design a crafting recipe on a 3x3 bench, add a function, fill in pack.mcmeta and your recipe file, debug a broken datapack log, then download a real datapack zip. No install and no account needed.

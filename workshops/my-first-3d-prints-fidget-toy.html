FAQ

Do I need a 3D printer?
No! You'll learn to design in TinkerCad. Once your design is ready, you can use an online 3D printing service to print it, visit a local makerspace, or print at home if you have a 3D printer.
Is TinkerCad really free?
Yes, completely free. Just sign up with an email account and you can start designing immediately.
How long does each episode take?
Each episode takes about 1-1.5 hours. You can pause anytime and come back later.
Can kids do this alone?
These are designed for parent and child together, but kids 10+ with tech experience can work through it independently.
What if my child has never done this before?
Perfect! Start at Episode 1. There's no previous knowledge needed. We'll teach you everything from the very first click.

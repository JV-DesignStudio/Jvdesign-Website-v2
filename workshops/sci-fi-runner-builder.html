Finished? Check your game

Reaching Step 10 is not the same as finishing. Use this checklist before you call your runner done.

  • The game starts and you can play it.
  • Movement, collisions and scoring all work.
  • At least one choice is yours: speed, size, lanes, shields or stars.
  • You playtested it and fixed one thing that felt wrong.
  • You know how to get back to your version.
How to keep it: your final settings are listed on Step 10. Write them down or take a screenshot so you can rebuild your runner later. The reference pack has the step notes for offline reading. It does not contain the game code. This builder runs in the page and does not save your game after you close it.

Explore, Complete, Create

Changing a setting is a good start, but writing the code is the real skill. Work through all three stages, then test your version in your own copy of the runner.

1. Explore

Change a value in Steps 1 to 9, then press Run and watch what happens. Try a low spawn rate, then a high one.

2. Complete

This line is broken. Fix it so collecting a star adds 50 points.

score = score - 50;
Show the fix
score = score + 50;
3. Create

Add a new pickup that gives the player one extra life. Write the code, then test it in your copy of runner.js.

Show one possible answer
if (pickup.type === 'life') {
  lives = lives + 1;
}

The reference pack is for reading the steps offline. To try your extra-life idea, change the spawn and collision code in this page's runner, then press Run.

Voxel Studio
Build a 3D voxel model in your browser

A little voxel maker for the Minecraft and 3D-print series. Spin a 3D grid, click to stack and paint cubes, read a broken log and choose the fix, then download a real OBJ mesh and a Minecraft .mcfunction of setblock commands.

🛠️ Open the Studio ← Back to all workshops
Make your own art and sounds: draw sprites in Pixel Studio and make music or sound effects in Sound Studio, then bring them into your project.
Ember says: a voxel is a 3D pixel. Click to stack cubes into a model, spin it to see every side, then take it into Minecraft as a build, or export the mesh for a 3D print. Your work saves automatically.

Build your model

Drag the canvas to spin the model, scroll to zoom, and click a cube to stack a new one on top. Use Erase to remove a cube.

Colours

Pick a colour, then build. Each colour becomes a real Minecraft block in the export.

      How this maps to real tools
      
        
      
          
      model.obj is a standard 3D mesh: vertices and faces. Open it in Blender, TinkerCAD or a slicer.
      
          
      model.mcfunction is a Minecraft datapack file. Drop it in a world and run /function to build your model in-game.
      
          
      Each colour becomes a real block, like grass_block or gold_block.
      
          
      Keep going: take the OBJ into Blender to smooth it, or the mcfunction into Minecraft to build it.

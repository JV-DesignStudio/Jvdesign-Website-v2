Studios, cheat sheets and templates

Browser studios, quick-reference sheets and starter files for when you want to go further.

Nothing to install
2 workshops
Roblox · Beginner · Ages 10+
Roblox Mod Studio
A free browser playground for the Roblox Studio series. Lay out an obby level on a grid, add real Lua scripts, read a broken Roblox Output log and choose the fix, then download a real .rbxmx model plus your scripts. No install and no account needed.
Roblox · Beginner · Ages 10+
Roblox Studio &amp; Lua Cheat Sheet
Printable Roblox Studio and Lua cheat sheet, keyboard shortcuts, common services, events, instance tricks and where scripts go. Free for kids and parents.

Everything else, plus every other engine, lives in the full workshop hub. Roblox Studio is a free download and you need a Roblox account to publish. The build canvases run in the browser.

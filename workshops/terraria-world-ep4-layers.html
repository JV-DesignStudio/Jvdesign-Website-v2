Two Layers
The tile you stand on, and the wall behind
Active
🎯
Goal for this step

See a world as a front layer and a back layer, stacked on every square.

  • 1The foreground is the tile you can stand on or bump into: grass, dirt, stone, wood.
  • 2The background wall sits behind that tile. It does not block you at all, you walk straight past it.
  • 3The wall is what makes a space count as a room, and it is what stops enemies spawning inside.
  • 4Every square can hold one foreground tile and one background wall, neatly stacked.

Placing Walls in TEdit
Switch to the wall brush
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Learn the wall brush, the tool that paints the back layer.

  • 1In TEdit you have two brushes: one for foreground tiles and one for walls.
  • 2Switch to the wall brush to paint the layer behind the tiles.
  • 3A wall can sit behind a solid tile too. You will not see it until you dig that tile away.
  • 4This is just like the Wall mode button in the browser Studio, so try it there first if you like.

Make a Safe Room
Walls keep enemies out
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Turn an empty space into a safe room by filling it with walls.

  • 1Dig out a small room, a few tiles wide and tall, down in the ground.
  • 2Switch to the wall brush and fill the whole room with walls, floor to ceiling.
  • 3Every open square needs a wall. One bare square is enough for an enemy to sneak in.
  • 4Drop in a torch and a door, and you have a safe little room to call home.

Natural Walls Underground
Dirt and stone walls already there
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Understand the natural walls the world already placed for you.

  • 1Underground, the game already places natural walls: dirt walls near the top and stone walls deeper down.
  • 2These natural walls are why most caves do not spawn enemies on every single tile.
  • 3You can place a natural wall yourself by picking dirt wall or stone wall with the wall brush.
  • 4Natural walls look rougher than crafted walls, so you can always tell them apart.

Common Wall Mistakes
Missing walls, or walls you did not want
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Spot the two wall mistakes and know how to fix each one.

  • 1Mistake one: a missing wall. One bare square and enemies can spawn right inside your room.
  • 2Mistake two: walls where you do want enemies. An enemy farm needs open squares with no walls.
  • 3To fix a missing wall, switch to the wall brush and fill that bare square.
  • 4To let enemies spawn on purpose, remove the wall with the eraser and leave the square bare.

Layers and Depth
Surface rooms vs underground rooms
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Use depth to decide where a room belongs and how it should feel.

  • 1The surface is the top layer of the world, where you spawn and can see the sky.
  • 2Underground rooms sit below the surface, tucked inside dirt and stone.
  • 3Surface rooms feel bright and open. Underground rooms feel cosy and safe.
  • 4Plan for the depth: big windows up top, tight walls and lamps down below.

Start Here - Pick Your Engine

Not sure where to begin? Choose the engine that fits your device and interest. Each track shows 3 workshops in order with time and skills. All free, all in the browser where possible, no account needed to start.

🐱
Scratch
Browser · Ages 6+ · No install · Best first engine
  1. 1. Don't Drop It! Fruit Catch
    Catch falling fruit with a basket. Learn sprites, keyboard input and scoring.
    ⏱ 8 steps · ~1 hrSkill: sprites, movement, score
  2. 2. Find the Exit! Maze
    Guide a sprite through walls, collect keys and add a timer with levels.
    ⏱ 11 steps · ~1.5 hrsSkill: walls, collision, levels
  3. 3. Jump & Run! Platformer
    Build a scrolling platformer with gravity, platforms, coins and a finish flag.
    ⏱ 10 steps · ~1.5 hrsSkill: gravity, scrolling, collectibles

Godot

  1. 1. GDScript Essentials

    ⏱ 6 steps · ~1 hrSkill: GDScript basics

    Build your first platformer scene, player movement and camera.
    ⏱ 8 steps · ~2 hrsSkill: scenes, physics, input

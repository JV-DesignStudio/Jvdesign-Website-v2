Questions from Parents

Do I need any coding experience?
None at all. Scratch uses coloured drag-and-drop blocks instead of typed code there's nothing to type or spell correctly. Each workshop is written for a grown-up and child reading together, with every step explained clearly.
What age is it suitable for?
Episode 1 works well from age 6 with a parent. Later episodes involve more blocks and logic and suit ages 7-8+ better. Confident readers can do more independently from around age 8.
Do I need to download anything?
No Scratch runs entirely in the browser at scratch.mit.edu. Nothing to install. It works on Windows, Mac, Chromebook and most tablets.
Do I need a Scratch account?
You can build without an account, but a free Scratch account (for children under 13, a parent creates it) lets you save projects and share them online so friends and family can play.
Do I need to do episodes in order?
No each episode stands alone. But if it's your first time with Scratch, Episode 1 (Fruit Catch) is the gentlest start and needs very few blocks.
Is it really free?
Yes all 6 episodes, forever. No ads, no paywall, no sign-up required. Scratch itself is also completely free, made by MIT.

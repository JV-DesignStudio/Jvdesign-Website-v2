🎒 Your backpack
Everything you earn and make is saved on this browser, on this device only - it is not uploaded to the internet. That means two things: your stuff is private, and you need to back it up yourself.

What gets saved here: your XP, badges, certificates and best scores are saved separately from any artwork or game projects you build - both are stored locally, but artwork lives inside each project's own save slot.

When you might lose it: clearing your browser history or cookies, using a private or incognito window, a school IT reset, or switching to a different browser or device can all wipe your progress and it cannot be undone.

Keep it safe: click Download my backpack to save a file with all your XP, badges, scores and artwork. You can open that file here any time to restore everything - or paste the code to move to another device.

Why Tunnels Help
Travel fast and hide from danger
Active
🎯
Goal for this step

Understand why a tunnel is worth the digging.

  • 1A tunnel is a path you dig through the ground. It lets you move from one place to another without climbing over every hill.
  • 2Most surface enemies cannot reach you the same way underground, so a tunnel can be a safer route.
  • 3A tunnel also links your base to far away places, so you spend less time walking and more time building.
  • 4Ember's tip: plan a tunnel like a road. Where does it start, where does it end, and what is in the way?

Dig a Tunnel in TEdit
Remove tiles to make a path
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Dig a clean tunnel by removing tiles in TEdit.

  • 1In TEdit, every square is a tile. To dig, you change solid tiles into empty space.
  • 2Pick a starting point and a direction. Dig in a straight line so the tunnel is easy to walk.
  • 3One tile high makes a quick crawl, three tiles high feels roomy and easy to move in.
  • 4Work in small stretches and save often. A long tunnel is just lots of short ones joined together.

Keep the Tunnel Safe

Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Keep your tunnel safe so enemies do not appear.

  • 1An empty tunnel with no background wall can let enemies spawn right next to you.
  • 2Place background walls along the tunnel, the same way you would in a room.
  • 3With the walls in place, the tunnel counts as safe space and stays clear.
  • 4This is the same idea from Episode 2: a wall turns empty space into a safe room.

Travel Made Easy
Ropes, platforms and rails
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Make travel along the tunnel quick and easy.

  • 1A tall empty tunnel is hard to climb, so add help to move through it.
  • 2Ropes let you climb straight up or down through a vertical shaft.
  • 3Platforms let you hop up ledges, and rails let a minecart carry you along a long tunnel.
  • 4Pick what fits: ropes for up and down, platforms for steps, rails for long trips.

Light the Way
Torches so you can see
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Light the tunnel so you can always see.

  • 1A dark tunnel is easy to get lost in, and you cannot see danger coming.
  • 2Place torches every few tiles along the path and in any side rooms.
  • 3You can also use glowsticks or lamps if you prefer a different look.
  • 4Good light makes a tunnel feel like a place, not just a hole.

Dig at Safe Depths
Watch for water and lava
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Dig at safe depths and watch out for hazards.

  • 1As you dig deeper, the world gets more dangerous. Stay aware of how far down you are.
  • 2If you break into a pocket of water, it can flood your tunnel in seconds.
  • 3Lava is even worse. One wrong tile can pour lava straight onto your path.
  • 4Dig slowly near caves and keep a way out. If you spot water or lava, stop and block the gap first.

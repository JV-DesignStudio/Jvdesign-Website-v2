TinkerCAD Mod Studio
Build a 3D model in your browser

A little model maker for the 3D-print series. Lay out 3D shapes on a grid, tick the model steps you want, read a broken log and choose the fix, then download a real STL you can import into TinkerCAD or send to a 3D printer.

🛠️ Open the Studio ← Back to all workshops
Make your own art and sounds: draw sprites in Pixel Studio and make music or sound effects in Sound Studio, then bring them into your project.
Ember says: a 3D print starts as a model made of triangles. This studio turns your grid into those triangles and writes a real STL file, the same kind TinkerCAD and every 3D printer use. Your work saves automatically.

Lay out your scene

Pick an object below, then click squares on the grid to place it. Put the Player where you start and a Goal at the end. Click a square again, or use the eraser, to remove it.

Objects

Your scene so far

Each shape becomes a solid in your 3D model. The mini map below is a bird's eye view of where they sit.

What is in the scene

    Add scripts

    A step changes your whole model, like sitting it flat or adding a base. Tick the ones you want and the Studio applies them. Start with Sit flat and Fit the bed so it is ready to print.

    Your scripts

    Each ticked step is applied when the model is built.

      How this maps to real 3D printing
      
        
      
          
      model.stl is a standard mesh: a list of triangles that describe your model.
      
          
      Sit flat drops the model so the lowest point is at z=0, ready for the printer bed.
      
          
      Fit the bed keeps the model inside a 100 mm print bed.
      
          
      Import it: in TinkerCAD, choose Import and pick model.stl (or open it in a slicer).
      
          
      Print it: ask a grown-up, then slice and print.

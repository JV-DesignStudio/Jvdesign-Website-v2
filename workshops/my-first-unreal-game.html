Questions & Answers

Do I need to know how to code?
No. All 3 episodes use Blueprints Unreal's visual scripting system. You drag and connect nodes instead of writing code. It's perfect for beginners and kids who want results fast.
Is Unreal Engine really free?
Yes. Unreal Engine 5 is completely free to download and use. Epic only takes a small royalty if your game earns over $1 million in revenue something you won't need to worry about while learning!
My computer is slow will it run Unreal Engine?
UE5 is demanding. You'll need at least 16 GB of RAM and a dedicated graphics card. If your machine struggles, consider trying Godot or GameMaker which run on much lower-spec computers.
What age is it suitable for?
Episodes 1 and 2 work from age 10 with adult support during setup. Episode 3 involves more Blueprint complexity and suits ages 11-12+ who are comfortable following longer instructions.
Can I publish my Unreal game?
Yes! Unreal can export to Windows, Mac, consoles and mobile. You can also package your game as an executable to share with friends.
Is it really free?
All 3 episodes, forever. No ads, no paywall, no sign-up. Unreal Engine 5 itself is free to download and use for learning and hobby projects.

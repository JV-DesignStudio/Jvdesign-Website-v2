Before You Start

What You Need to Install

Unreal Engine 5 is completely free. You'll need a powerful-enough computer and the Epic Games Launcher we walk you through setup in Episode 1. Episodes 1-6 use Blueprints only; Episode 7 introduces C++.

🔺
Epic Games Launcher
Download the free launcher, sign in with a free Epic account and install Unreal Engine 5. The engine itself is free to use.
✓ Free · epicgames.com
💻
A Decent PC
UE5 is demanding. You'll want at least 16 GB RAM, a decent GPU and an SSD. It doesn't run well on old or low-spec machines.
Windows 10/11 recommended
📐
Blueprints First
Episodes 1-6 use Blueprints UE5's visual scripting system. You connect nodes instead of typing code. Episode 7 onward introduces real C++ once you're ready.
✓ Visual Scripting
⏱️
Patience at First
UE5 takes a while to install and compile shaders on first launch. Set aside extra time on Day 1 it's worth it once it's running!
✓ Worth the Wait

Questions & Answers

Do I need to know how to code?
Not to start. Episodes 1-6 use Blueprints Unreal's visual scripting system where you drag and connect nodes instead of writing code. Episode 7 introduces real C++ once you're comfortable with the engine.
Is Unreal Engine really free?
Yes. Unreal Engine 5 is completely free to download and use. Epic only takes a small royalty if your game earns over $1 million in revenue something you won't need to worry about while learning!
My computer is slow will it run Unreal Engine?
UE5 is demanding. You'll need at least 16 GB of RAM and a dedicated graphics card. If your machine struggles, consider trying Godot or GameMaker which run on much lower-spec computers.
What age is it suitable for?
Episodes 1-4 work from age 10 with adult support during setup. Episodes 5-6 involve more Blueprint complexity and suit ages 10-11+. Episodes 7-9 introduce C++ and multiplayer and suit ages 12+ who are comfortable following longer instructions.
Can I publish my Unreal game?
Yes! Unreal can export to Windows, Mac, consoles and mobile. You can also package your game as an executable to share with friends.
Is it really free?
All 9 episodes, forever. No ads, no paywall, no sign-up. Unreal Engine 5 itself is free to download and use for learning and hobby projects.

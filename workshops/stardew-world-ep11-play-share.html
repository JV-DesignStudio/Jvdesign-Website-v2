Goal for this step

Open play mode and walk your world with collision.

  • 1Open the Stardew Mod Studio and go to the Map editor tab.
  • 2Click ⛶ Play full screen.
  • 3Walk with the arrow keys (or WASD), or the on-screen pad on a tablet.
  • 4You cannot walk through walls or water. That is the whole point of the rules you set.

Collision Comes From Rules
Open a path the rules had blocked
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Use the rules panel to fix a blocked path.

  • 1In play mode, find a tile you want to cross but cannot.
  • 2Close play, select that tile in the palette, and look at the Rules panel.
  • 3Set Passable to T (true) and paint the tile again.
  • 4Play again. The path is open. That is how the real game feels tiles too.
✏️

Goal for this step

Save your world with a name so you can come back to it.

  • 1In the Studio, find the My worlds card.
  • 2Type a name, like Sunny Farm, and click Save.
  • 3Your world appears in the gallery as a little picture. You can Load or delete it any time.
  • 4Saved worlds stay on your device, so they are yours and no account is needed.

Share a Link

Locked

Goal for this step

Copy a share link and open a shared world.

  • 1Click 🔗 Share next to your world (or the Share button at the top of My worlds).
  • 2A link is copied. It carries your whole map inside it.
  • 3Paste it into a chat or email. Anyone who opens it sees your world in the Studio.
  • 4Ask a grown-up before you share a link online.

Download a World Page

Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Make a standalone page that shows your world.

  • 1Click ⬇ World page (.html) in the My worlds card.
  • 2You get a single file that opens in any browser and shows your world.
  • 3It needs no internet and no game, so it is a keepsake you can send or print.
  • 4This is evidence of your work, ready to show a teacher or family.

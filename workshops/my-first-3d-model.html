Questions & Answers

Do I need any art or 3D experience?
No. Episode 1 starts with installing Blender and learning to move the camera. Every tool is introduced one step at a time.
Is Blender really free?
Yes. Blender is free and open-source, and is used professionally on films, adverts and games. There is no paid tier.
What will I learn?
The full 3D pipeline: modelling, character creation, rigging, animation, materials and textures, lighting, and composing a complete rendered scene.
What age is it suitable for?
Ages 10+ with adult help at first. Blender is keyboard-heavy, so later episodes suit learners comfortable with shortcuts.
Will my computer run it?
Almost certainly. Blender runs on modest laptops. A dedicated GPU makes final renders faster but every workshop works without one.
Is it really free?
All 7 episodes, forever. No ads, no paywall, no sign-up needed.

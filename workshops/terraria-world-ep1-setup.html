You Need the Game
What world editing needs, and what it costs
Active
🎯
Goal for this step

Understand exactly what you need before you touch anything.

  • 1Terraria is a paid game for PC, Mac and Linux. You need to own it before you can play or edit worlds.
  • 2The tools are free: tModLoader (the mod loader) and TEdit (the world editor) cost nothing.
  • 3Every world you make lives inside one folder: Terraria / Worlds (or the tModLoader Worlds folder).
  • 4Editing never touches the original if you copy the world first. A backup means you can always undo.

Grown-up note: tModLoader is a free, official tool. Once installed, it appears in your Steam library (or as its own launcher), so you can start either normal Terraria or the modded one.
  • 1tModLoader is the free mod loader. It runs alongside your normal game and does not change it.

  • 3When it opens, you get a mods menu and your own worlds list.
  • 4From now on, you can launch normal Terraria or tModLoader, whichever you like.

Install the free editor you will use to shape a world.

Find & Back Up a World
Locate your worlds, then copy one
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Find the Worlds folder and copy a world before you touch it.

  • 1Find your Worlds folder using the paths below. It sits inside your Terraria (or tModLoader) documents folder.
  • 2Inside, you will see your worlds as .wld files, plus a matching .wld.bak backup.
  • 3Before editing, copy the world and give the copy a new name, like TestWorld.
  • 4Work only on the copy. If anything goes wrong, the original is safe.
Where the Worlds folder lives
Windows:  Documents\My Games\Terraria\Worlds
tModLoader: Documents\My Games\Terraria\tModLoader\Worlds
Mac:      ~/Library/Application Support/Terraria/Worlds
Linux:    ~/.local/share/Terraria/Worlds

Read the Load Log
Spot a healthy load versus an error
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Learn to read the log, so every future fix is quick.

  • 1When tModLoader starts, it logs what it loaded. Normal lines are grey or white. That is a healthy load.
  • 2A red line or the word Exception means something failed. Nine times out of ten it is a version mismatch or a nested folder.
  • 3Look for the line that names your world loading.
  • 4If the world loads with no red lines, your setup is ready. That is the whole goal of Episode 1.
What a healthy log looks like
[tModLoader] Loading world MyWorld.wld
[tModLoader] Loaded 0 mods
[tModLoader] World loaded, no errors

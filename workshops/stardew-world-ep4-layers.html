What Is a Layer?
A stack of transparent sheets
Active
🎯
Goal for this step

Understand that a map is layers stacked on top of each other.

  • 1A map is not one picture. It is several layers stacked up.
  • 2Each layer holds its own tiles. Lower layers show first, higher layers draw on top.
  • 3In Tiled, the Layers panel lists them. The top of the list draws over the bottom.
  • 4If something is hidden, you painted it on a layer that is covered.

Back: the Ground
Grass, dirt, water and flooring
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Know that Back is the bottom layer for the ground.

  • 1The Back layer is the ground: grass, dirt, sand, water and floors.
  • 2Almost every change starts here, because everything else sits on top of it.
  • 3If you paint the ground on a higher layer, it covers trees and buildings. Always ground on Back.

Goal for this step

Know what Buildings and Paths are for.

  • 1Buildings holds houses, sheds, walls and objects that sit on the ground.
  • 2Paths is for walkable flooring such as stone paths and decks.
  • 3Buildings draw over the ground but behind the player, so you can walk in front of them.

Front: Over the Player
Tree tops and things you walk behind
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Know why some tiles go on Front.

  • 1The Front layer draws over the player.
  • 2Tree tops, roofs and arches go here, so you can walk behind them and they hide you.
  • 3Paint grass on Front by mistake and you get a green block that floats over you. That is the classic bug.

AlwaysFront & Draw Order
The very top, and the golden rule
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Meet the top layer and lock in the draw order.

  • 1AlwaysFront is the very top: weather, mist and full-screen effects.
  • 2The usual draw order, bottom to top: Back → Buildings → Paths → Front → AlwaysFront.
  • 3Golden rule: ground low, objects middle, over-the-player high.
  • 4When something looks wrong, ask "which sheet is it on?" before anything else.

Practice: Move a Tile
Fix a misplaced tile on purpose
Locked
🎯
Goal for this step

Prove you can move a tile to the correct layer.

  • 1Open your map, pick a tile, and paint a patch on the Front layer on purpose.
  • 2Save, load the game and see the floating patch. That is your bug on purpose.
  • 3Back in Tiled, delete those tiles with the eraser, then paint them on Back instead.
  • 4Save and load again: fixed. You can now debug by layer.

Finish in Godot

Turn your preview into a real bakery

The canvas is a live preview. To keep the game and play it in Godot, build the same scene in the free editor.

  1. Download the Biscuit Tycoon starter project.
  2. Open Godot 4 from godotengine.org, choose Import and pick project.godot.
  3. Press F5. The tin, labels, shop buttons, golden timer and signals are already wired.
  4. Open bakery.gd and follow the In Godot panel under each step. The code is the same code you wrote in the canvas.
  5. Check the tap, the auto tapper, both shop buttons and the golden biscuit, then open Project > Export to keep your game.

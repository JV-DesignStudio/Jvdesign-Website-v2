Stardust's five moves

Don't just copy it. Own it.

Copying the finished code proves it runs. Explaining and changing it proves you understand it. Work through these five moves.

  1. 1. See it Pip Watch the finished build work before you type anything. Notice one thing it does that you want to keep. Open it
  2. 2. Understand it Lumo Read why the important lines are there before you copy them. If you can say what a line does in your own words, you understand it. Open it
  3. 3. Build it Ember Type or assemble the guided version yourself. Keep the reference solution closed unless a step stops you. Open it
  4. 4. Fix it Echo Try Echo's Debug It challenge. Find the bug from the clues before you open the answer. Open it
  5. 5. Own it Stardust Change one thing the guide never asked for: changing one rule, level or value and testing what happens. If your version runs, it is yours. Open it

Echo's recovery check

Stuck? Try this

These are the four things that most often stop the Platformer Builder. Check the symptom first, then try the fix.

The player falls through the floor

What you'll see: the sprite keeps falling.

Fix: Check the player bottom edge against the platform top and snap the player to the platform.

Jumping only works once

What you'll see: the player cannot jump again after landing.

Fix: Reset the grounded flag when the player is standing on a platform.

The camera leaves the player behind

What you'll see: the player walks off screen.

Fix: Move the camera by the same amount as the player and draw the world relative to it.

Platforms are in the wrong place

What you'll see: the level looks different after reload.

Fix: Keep the level data in one array and draw from that array, not from the DOM.

Still stuck? Reread the step, open the cheat sheet, or ask an adult to sit with you.

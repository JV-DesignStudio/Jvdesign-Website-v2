Echo's Debug It

Debug It: The player who walks through walls

The character moves, but it slides straight through the level walls instead of stopping.

extends CharacterBody2D

var speed = 200.0

func _process(delta):
    var direction = Input.get_axis("ui_left", "ui_right")
    position.x += direction * speed * delta
  1. What did you expect? The player stops when it reaches a wall.
  2. What happened instead? The player passes through the wall and keeps going off the edge of the level.
  3. What evidence tells you where the problem is? Print position.x every frame. It keeps increasing past the wall's x position, so nothing is blocking the movement.
Hint 1: a nudge

A CharacterBody2D has a built-in movement call that handles collisions. Are you using it?

Hint 2: what to look for

position.x += moves the node directly and ignores physics. move_and_slide() reads the velocity variable and stops the body at collisions, which is why every CharacterBody2D example sets velocity first.

Full answer

Set the velocity and let the engine move the body: 

velocity.x = direction * speed
velocity.y += gravity * delta
move_and_slide()

Try the hints before the answer. Finding the bug is the skill, the fix is the easy part.

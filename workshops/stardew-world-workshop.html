Stardew World Workshop
Build Your Own World

Start with the farm you know, then shape it, decorate it and grow it into a brand-new place nobody has seen before. You will edit real Stardew maps in Tiled, then package your world as your own mod and import it into the game.

🛡️
Two ways to learn this: the full series needs the paid Stardew Valley game plus a free mod loader install, so it is a home-computer job best with a grown-up. But you can start right now, with no install, in the browser studio, on any computer including a school one.
🐯
Ember says: we build in small steps, and each one ends with something you can actually see in the game. No giant leaps. By the last episode you will have your own world that you can load, save and share.
🛠️ Open the Stardew Mod Studio A browser playground for this series: paint a map with layers and tile rules, walk it to test, try a doorway warp, build your manifest and content files, debug a SMAPI log and download your mod. No install, works on a school computer. Start building →
⏱️ Your first 15 minutes (no install)
  1. Open the Mod Studio.
  2. Paint some grass, water and a path on the map, then add a building.
  3. Press Start walk test and try to walk into the water. It should block you.
  4. On the Download your mod tab, download your world as a zip. That is a real mod folder.

Then, when you have the game and a grown-up to help with the install, start Episode 1 and take it into Stardew for real.

The 12 Episodes

Follow them in order. Each episode is about 45 minutes and adds one new skill.

Episode 1 · Beginner
Get Set Up 🌱
Install SMAPI, Content Patcher and Tiled, find your Mods folder and read the SMAPI console.
Episode 2 · Beginner
Inside a Mod 📦
Tour the Mods folder and learn how SMAPI loads a mod from its manifest and content files.
Episode 3 · Beginner
Your First Change 🧱
Copy the farm map, open it in Tiled, change one tile, and see it in the game.
Episode 4 · Improver
Layers Explained 🗂️
Back, Buildings, Front and Paths: why the order of layers changes what you see and walk on.
Episode 5 · Improver
Tile Properties 🚶
Make a tile walkable, tillable or water, so the game knows how the player treats it.
Episode 6 · Improver
Build & Decorate 🌳
Place a building, add trees, paths and decorations to turn a plain plot into a place.
Episode 7 · Improver
Warps & Doors 🚪
Add an entrance and an exit so the player can walk between areas without falling through the world.
Episode 8 · Advanced
A Brand-New Area 🏝️
Build a small new location from a blank map and connect it to the world with a warp.
Episode 9 · Advanced
Package Your Mod 📦
Write a manifest and content file, then export your world as a mod you can share.
Episode 10 · Advanced
Fix It & Finish 🏆
Read the SMAPI log, fix the common map mistakes, then finish your own world challenge.
Episode 11 · Capstone
Play & Share Your World 🏆
Run around your world with real collision, save and name it, share a link and download a world page.
Episode 12 · Design
Design Your World 🎨
Think like a level designer: purpose, guide the eye, flow, balance and detail.

Try it now: Sketch your world

Plan a layout in your browser before you open Tiled. Pick a tile, click or drag on the grid to paint, then download your plan. This bit needs no install and no game, so you can start right now.

Tip: paint the ground first, then paths, then trees and buildings on top. "Save for Tiled" gives you a .tmx file you can open in the Tiled editor and keep building in Episode 3.

🧰 What you need

A computer (Windows, Mac or Linux), the paid Stardew Valley game, and a grown-up to help with the first install.

🧑‍🌾 The badge

Finish all 12 episodes to earn the Farm Architect badge on your progress page.

📦 Starter files

Every episode is in the starter pack. All the code blocks also have a Copy button.

✦ The Process

How a game gets made

💡
The Idea
A feeling you want to create. What should it feel like to play?
✏️
On Paper
Rules, mechanics, how scoring works. No code yet.
🔨
First Build
Ugly but working. Can you play it? Does it feel right?
🔁
Iterate
Play it. Break it. Fix it. Play it again.
🚀
Ship It
Put it live. Real players find things you never will alone.

Lumo's Firefly Shooter went through 3 major versions before the board-clear mechanic felt right.

🎮

✦ The Process

How a book gets made

💜
The Feeling
What real emotion needs to be seen? What do children feel but can't name?
🦊
The Character
Who feels this feeling? What kind of creature shows it best?
✍️
The Rhyme
32 pages. ~700 words. Every syllable must earn its place.
🎨
The Art
Illustrations that carry more weight than the words alone.
📚
The Book
A thing a child holds. A feeling they recognise. A conversation that starts.
Picture book = 32 pages ~700 words max Rhyme scheme matters Ages 3–8

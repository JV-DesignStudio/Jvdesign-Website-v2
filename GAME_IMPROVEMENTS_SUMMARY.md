- **Features:** 
  - Exponential growth mechanics

  - Cost scaling with ownership
  - Real-time passive income
  - Full XP & achievement integration
- **Visual Style:** Warm, cozy browns and creams with floating animations

### 2. Neon Tiles
- **Type:** Fast-Paced Rhythm Game
- **Gameplay:** Tap neon tiles as they light up, build streaks
- **Features:**
  - Accelerating difficulty (speed increases with score)
  - Combo/streak tracking
  - 9-tile grid with smooth animations
  - Cascade mechanics
  - Full XP & achievement rewards
- **Visual Style:** Neon cyberpunk (blues, cyans, purples)

### 3. Bubble Pop Galaxy
- **Type:** Match-3 Bubble Popper
- **Gameplay:** Tap connected bubbles of same color, create combos
- **Features:**
  - Flood-fill matching algorithm
  - Exponential combo multipliers
  - Dynamic grid (30 bubbles, 5 colors)
  - Smooth pop animations
  - Full XP & achievement integration
- **Visual Style:** Dark space theme with vibrant bubble gradients

VOIDRUSH

The void is alive — and it's hungry. Pilot your ship through the neon dark, collect fuel to survive, and pray it doesn't catch you.

🚀 1 Player ♾️ Endless ⛽ Fuel-Based Survival 👾 Void Entity 🆓 Free to Play
HOW TO PLAY ↓
FREE TO PLAY
✦ How to Play

Survive the void

Three lanes. Dwindling fuel. A creature made of nothing that wants to swallow everything. You fly.

⛽
Collect Fuel
Your ship burns fuel constantly. Pick up canisters to keep flying — run dry and the void catches you instantly.
💰
Grab Credits
Credit chips and rare gems litter the lanes. Snag them to build your score and unlock upgrades in future runs.
☄️
Dodge Hazards
Asteroids, void mines, and debris fill the lanes. A hit drains fuel and pushes the void closer to catching you.
👾
Outrun the Void Entity
The further you fly and the lower your fuel gets, the more the entity wakes up. Keep it at bay or face the end.

✦ The Threat

What hunts you

The void isn't empty — it's watching. Every second you fly, it grows stronger.

☄️
Asteroids
Fast-moving rocks. Dodge or eat a fuel penalty and wake the void up faster.
💀
Void Mines
Stationary and devastating. Direct contact supercharges the void meter.
🪨
Debris Fields
Cluttered lanes of junk. Hard to read, easy to clip. Stay sharp.
👁️
The Void Entity
It has eyes. It has tentacles. The lower your fuel, the closer it gets. Don't let it close its grip.

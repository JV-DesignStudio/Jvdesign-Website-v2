## 🎥 How to Create the Instagram Post

### Option 1: Quick & Easy (Use SVG directly)
1. Open the SVG file in your web browser
2. Right-click → "Save as PNG" (or screenshot)
3. Upload to Instagram
4. Copy the caption from above
5. Add hashtags
6. Post!

### Option 2: Professional Export (Using design software)
1. Open SVG in Figma, Adobe XD, or Illustrator
2. Export as PNG (1080x1080)
3. Adjust size/crop if needed
4. Upload to Instagram
5. Add caption + hashtags

### Option 3: Use Instagram's Native Tools
1. Screenshots the SVG (open in browser, zoom to fit)
2. Upload directly to Instagram
3. Use Instagram's built-in filters if desired
4. Add caption text

## 💡 Engagement Tips

### Caption Strategy
- **Ask questions** ("What's your favorite character?", "Which quest will you try first?")
- **Use emojis** to break up text
- **Keep it conversational** — not corporate
- **Include CTA** — "Try it free", "Share your score", "Tag a friend"

### Posting Tips
- **Post when your audience is active** (typically 6-9 PM, 12 PM for lunch scrolling)
- **Use Stories to promote posts** ("Check out our latest game features!")
- **Pin top-performing post** for 3-7 days
- **Respond to comments quickly** (increases reach)
- **Repost user-generated content** if people share their scores

### Image Optimization
- All 5 graphics are already 1080x1080 (Instagram square standard)
- SVG format means they'll look crisp on any device
- No additional resizing needed

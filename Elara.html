✦ The Story

A magical adventure
with a real lesson

Elara is just like any curious child but one day her tablet opens a portal to the Wire-Web, a vivid digital world full of mystery and hidden danger. To find her way home she'll need to learn about safe passwords, careful sharing, and trusting her instincts online.

Written to spark conversations between parents, teachers, and young readers, Elara & The Wire-Web Key makes cyber safety feel like an adventure — not a lecture.

✦ A Note from the Author

Why I wrote Elara's story

Children are growing up in a digital world, and I wanted to give them and the adults in their lives a way to talk about online safety that feels fun, not frightening. Elara's adventure is my way of turning a scary subject into a story worth telling.

Every page is crafted with care, from the story to the illustrations. I hope it sparks great conversations in your home or classroom.

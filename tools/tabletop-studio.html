How to play

  1. Paint. Pick a colour, then tap or drag on the board. Fill floods a whole area.
  2. Add a piece. Pick a character or a prop, then drag it onto the board. Tap a counter to raise or lower its number.
  3. Make cards. In Cards, write your own card, add it to the deck, then draw it. Tap a card on the board to flip it.
  4. Play. Switch to Play, drag pieces anywhere, and use Play aids to track rounds, turns and your rules.
  5. Save. It saves as you go. Keep several boards, print one, or save a picture and a play sheet.

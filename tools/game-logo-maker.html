Free Game Logo Maker for indie devs

Create a polished game logo right in your browser , no design skills, no sign-up, no watermarks. Pick a style like pixel, neon, horror or fantasy, type your title and tagline, tweak colours and effects, then download a high-res PNG ready for Steam, itch.io, Game Jolt or social media. Everything renders locally on your device, and every logo you make is yours to use commercially.

How it works

  1. Pick a template or style preset (pixel, neon, horror, sci-fi…).
  2. Type your game title, tagline and icon.
  3. Fine-tune fonts, colours, glow, outline and background.
  4. Download a PNG , or copy a share link to re-edit later.

FAQ

Is it really free?
Yes , every style, font and effect is free, with no account required.
Can I use my logo commercially?
Absolutely. Use it on store pages, thumbnails, trailers and merch.
What sizes can I export?
800×280 banner, 512×512 icon, 1280×640 social header and 630×500 , with or without a transparent background.
Is my artwork uploaded anywhere?
No. Rendering, saving and sharing all happen inside your browser.

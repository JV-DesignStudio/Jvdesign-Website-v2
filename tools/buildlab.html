🧱 Welcome to BuildLab

Build anything with blocks, right in your browser. No install, no account — everything stays on your device. Use it to learn 3D, then export your creations to Roblox, Blender or Unreal Engine!

💡 Learn — start with a template (Robot, Tree, Sword...) to see how blocks build up a 3D object
➕ Add — tap or click the grid to place blocks
🎨 Craft — paint, resize, rotate and pick shapes
🖥️ Export — Roblox (.rbxm), Blender (.glb/.obj) or Unreal (.obj)
🔗 Share — send your whole build as a single link
Right-drag = orbit · Scroll = zoom · Middle-drag = pan · Click = place
G/R/S = gizmo · Ctrl+Z/Y = undo/redo · Ctrl+C/V = copy/paste
Ctrl+D = duplicate · Ctrl+A = select all · Ctrl+G = group

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the Map Generator really free?

Yes , completely free, no account, no watermark, and everything runs locally in your browser.

Can I use the maps I make commercially?

Yes. Every map you generate or edit is yours to use, including in commercial games, apps and products.

What's in the JSON export?

Map dimensions, tile size, the full tile grid, human-readable tile names, room rectangles for dungeons, plus the seed and mode , enough to rebuild the exact map as a Godot TileMap or Unity Tilemap.

Do identical seeds give identical maps?

Yes. Generation is deterministic per seed and settings, so the same inputs always reproduce the same map , ideal for sharing layouts with your team.

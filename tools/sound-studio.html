🎛️ Welcome to Audio Studio

One studio, three modes. Compose a song, build a beat, or make a single sound effect. Each row is an instrument and each square is one beat.

How it works
  • 1 PickChoose Music, Drums or SFX at the top of the page.
  • 2 TapClick squares in the grid to switch beats on. Press ▶ Play to hear your loop.
  • 3 ShapeClick a track name to change its sound on the right, or tap a drum pad to add drums.
  • 4 Export⬇ WAV downloads your loop or sound. Your work autosaves as you go.

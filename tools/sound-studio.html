One studio, three ways to make sound

Audio Studio is one tool with two jobs. Music builds songs and loops, Drums helps you make a quick beat, and SFX makes short game sounds like jumps, hits, coins and clicks. The old SFX Studio now lives here, in the SFX tab.

  • Download WAV gives you a file you can drop into Game Maker, Godot, Unity or Scratch.
  • Send to Game Maker imports the sound in one click.
  • Save keeps your work on this device. Use Project to save or share a copy.

🎛️ Welcome to Audio Studio

One studio, three modes. Compose a song, build a beat, or make a single sound effect. Each row is an instrument and each square is one beat.

Pick a starting point
Start with a game moment
How it works
  • 1 PickChoose Music, Drums or SFX at the top of the page.
  • 2 TapClick squares in the grid to switch beats on, or press ✨ Surprise me and change what it made. Press ▶ Play to hear your loop.
  • 3 ShapeTap a melody square to cycle through notes that fit the key, or pick a sound like Beepy or Spacey on the right.
  • 4 Export⬇ WAV downloads your loop or sound. Your work autosaves as you go.

Change the key or mood at the top. In tune keeps every note sounding good; switch to Advanced when you want every control.

For games

Loop WAV is cut to the exact bar length so it repeats with no gap. OGG is smaller for the web and school devices, but it records in real time, so Loop WAV is the exact offline copy. Both open straight in Godot, Defold and Unity, not just the Arcade Game Maker.

The sound kit packs your song loop, My Sounds and your current Sound Lab sound, with a sounds.json manifest and a README. The built-in pack is optional.

Game pack

Pick a sound for each slot. Empty slots are skipped. The pack adds sounds.json and a copy-paste game-sounds.js for the Arcade Game Maker.

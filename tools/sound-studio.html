🎛️ Welcome to Audio Studio

A multi-track step sequencer. Each row is an instrument, each square is one beat. Build a loop, shape the sound on the right, then export a WAV or send it to Arcade Game Maker.

How it works
  • 1 TapClick squares in the grid to switch beats on. Press ▶ Play to hear your loop.
  • 2 ShapeClick a track name, then use the right panel to change its wave, envelope and effects.
  • 3 NotesUse Set Notes per Step on melody tracks to pick pitches.
  • 4 Export⬇ WAV downloads the loop. Your work autosaves as you go.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the Level Designer really free?

Yes , completely free, no account, and no watermark on anything you export.

Can I use the levels I make commercially?

Yes. Your levels are yours, including use in commercial games, apps and products.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. There's a touch-first mobile layout, and you can install the Level Designer as an app on Android, iOS, Windows and macOS , it then works offline.

How do I turn my level into a real game?

Hit "Send to Game Maker" and your level flows straight into the free JVDS Arcade Game Maker , platforms, hazards and all. You can also export PNG or JSON for use in any engine.

How the Skin Editor works

A Minecraft skin is a small picture (64x64 pixels) that wraps around your player. Here is how to make one in a few minutes.

  1. Pick a base. Choose Classic, Blank or another template, or import a skin you already have.
  2. Paint. Use the pencil and quick colours. The 3D player updates as you paint so you can see the front, back and sides.
  3. Export. Press Export PNG and keep the file. Put it in your Minecraft skin folder to use in game.

Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and your skin is saved on this device.

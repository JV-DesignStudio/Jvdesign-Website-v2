Build a game character from parts, then stamp it onto a pixel canvas and paint your own details on top. Export a PNG, a spritesheet or send it straight to Arcade Game Maker.

How it works
  • 1 — ChoosePick a Template along the top, then a game role like Player or Final Boss.
  • 2 — TweakChange body, head, eyes and colours in the Parts list until it looks right.
  • 3 — StampPress Stamp to Frame 1, or Stamp All Poses for a ready-made animation.
  • 4 — PaintDraw your own details on the Paint layer, then export.

Stuck for ideas? Random builds a whole character for you.

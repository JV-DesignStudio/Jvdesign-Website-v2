🎬 Welcome to Sprite Animator

Turn a spritesheet into a playable animation. Slice it into frames, preview the motion, tune the timing, then export a GIF or an engine-ready pack.

How it works
  • 1 · LoadUpload a spritesheet PNG, or press Import from Pixel Studio to bring across what you drew there.
  • 2 · SliceSet the frame width and height, or hit Auto-detect frames and let it guess.
  • 3 · PreviewPress Play. Adjust FPS, or give single frames their own timing under Per-Frame Timing.
  • 4 · ExportAnimated GIF and Frames Sheet sit at the bottom of this panel; scroll it for JSON and ZIP packs.

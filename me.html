🎒 My backpack: take your saves with you

Everything you make and earn here is kept in this browser, on this device. There is no account, and nothing is sent to us. That is why backing up is your own job, and it only takes a minute.

💾 What is saved
Things you made: drawings, game projects, maps, stories and levels. Your progress: XP, level, day streak, best scores, workshops done, achievements and badges. They are separate lists, but one backpack file holds both.
📍 Where it is saved
Inside this browser, on this computer or tablet. It is not on our servers, so opening the site somewhere else will not find it on its own.
⚠️ What can wipe it
Clearing browsing data, cookies or site data. A private or incognito window closing. A different browser profile or computer. A school reset. A wipe cannot be undone, and nobody here can get it back for you.
🧰 How to get it back
Download your backpack, or copy the code, before you finish. On any computer, open the file or paste the code. It only brings back what was inside the backpack when you saved it, so save again after new work.

Echo says: download at the end of every session and keep the file on a USB stick, your school drive or in an email to yourself. If a file will not open, it is not a JVDS backpack and nothing on this device has been changed.

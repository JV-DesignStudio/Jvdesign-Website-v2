Make a Game on a School Chromebook

School computers are locked down, but you can still make a real game. Everything here runs in the browser with no installs, no admin rights, no paid software and no account. Open a tab and start.

Stardust says: if the school blocks downloads you are still fine. These workshops run entirely in the browser.

What it looks like

Why it's different

☕

No lives. No fail states.

The cafe never closes on you. Serve at your own pace. The day clock only ticks while you're actually playing - put your phone down and it pauses completely.

🌧

The real London sky

The cafe's light follows the actual time of day over London, and the window shows the real weather - sun, rain, fog or snow. Grey outside? The cafe feels cosier.

� -

A 30-chapter story

"A Year at the Cafe" unlocks one page at a time as you serve more guests - a gentle story about friendship, quiet days, and the chair that was always meant for someone.

✨

Today's Special

Every cafe day opens with a choice: Golden Hour, Slow Morning, Espresso Rush, Feast Day, and more. Six gentle twists - or play it straight. Always your call.

🏠

Decorate & prestige

Place lamps, plants, rugs and fairy lights. Prestige to open a bigger venue and choose permanent perks that stack - every new cafe plays a little differently.

📵

Plays offline

The cafe only reaches the internet for optional London weather and adverts. No accounts, no sign-ups. Your save stays on your device.

★ How to Play

Three rules, zero pressure

1

Swap & Match

Slide adjacent snack tiles to line up three or more of the same. Hand-drawn berries, mushrooms, algae crackers, and tiny pastries.

2

Read the Cravings

Each visiting friend has a craving meter at the top of the screen. Match their favourite and watch their happiness sparkle. Match their least favourite and they'll give you a soft little frown.

3

No Lives, Ever

Stuck? The board reshuffles itself. Tired? Pull up the Zen button and switch the music to rainfall. The cafe waits for you.

★ The Connected World

Two games, one little world

Cozy Cafe Match shares a vault with Sky High Squirt. Coins you earn here can be spent there. Hats unlocked there appear on the friends sipping tea here. It all happens through a tiny shared save file in your browser — no accounts, no logins.

🪙

Shared Coins

Dream Sparks earned in the cafe go straight into the same vault as the platformer's currency.

🎩

Cosmetic Bridge

Bought a wizard hat in Sky High Squirt? The fox is wearing it next time you open the cafe.

🏆

Cross-Game Unlocks

Match 100 algae tiles here to unlock the Chef's Toque over there. Little rewards that link both worlds together.

✦ The Story

A mountain, an axolotl, and one small try 🏔️

Deep in the Sunken Valley, a small pink-gilled axolotl named Echo faces her biggest challenge yet a towering mountain with a sparkle-sharp peak scratching stars in the sky. At the top grows a legendary flower that changes colour wherever you go.

Echo is a Junior Scout swimmer, nervous but determined. The mountain seems impossibly large. She trips, slides backwards, gets lost in mist. But with encouragement from the patient Shelly the Snail, and her own growing courage, Echo discovers that mountains aren't climbed all at once they're climbed one small try at a time.

When the obvious path fails, Echo learns to think differently zigzagging sideways when straight ahead won't work. When mist hides the peak, she follows only the next step her lantern lights. And when she finally reaches the top, the flower blooms with all her colours: pink for pride, blue for bravery, gold for joy.

📖 From the Manuscript · Pages 5–6
She gazed past the ponds to a mountain up high,
With a sparkle-sharp peak scratching stars in the sky.
"Up there," whispered stories, "a flower does grow,
That changes its colours wherever you go."
Echo breathed in. "I'll climb. Yes… I think that I might.
I'll try a small try then try with all my might."

✦ Echo's Journey

Five challenges on the mountain 🌿

Every stumble on Echo's path teaches her something new about resilience, creativity, and looking at problems differently.

😟
The First Step
The mountain looms. Echo's tummy ties knots. Her knees shake. But she takes one step anyway and then another.
"One step now… what's the next step for?"
🐌
Shelly's Wisdom
Shelly the Snail appears on a slippery rock, smiling slow and wide. "A mountain," she says, "is just steps stacked so."
"Try a small try… then try once more as you go."
💫
The Trip & Fall
TRIP-FLIP-TUMBLE! A root catches her toe and Echo lands in a dirt-puff cloud. She nearly gives up until she looks back down.
"I'm not at the start. I've climbed part of the land."
🔀
The Zigzag Idea
Loose gravel sends her sliding. Straight up won't work. Echo thinks differently she zigzags sideways in a wiggly-wig dance.
"Try a small try… and try a new kind."
🌫️
The Misty Peak
Thick mist swallows the path. Echo holds her lantern and decides she doesn't need the whole climb just the next step in the light.
"Just the next little step that my light lets me know."
✦ Characters

Meet Echo and Shelly 🦎🐌

🦎
Echo
The Axolotl · Junior Scout Swimmer
Pink-gilled and small, Echo is a Junior Scout swimmer with big dreams and very shaky knees. She's nervous but she doesn't let that stop her. Her greatest strength isn't her size or speed; it's her willingness to try, fall, think differently, and try again.
"Today," Echo whispered, her voice soft but sure, "I'll find my inner glow. I'll climb and explore."
🐌
Shelly
The Snail · Patient Guide
Shelly appears on a slick rock with a hum-honey sound, smiling slow and wide. Patient, warm, and full of the kind of wisdom that only comes from taking life one careful step at a time. She doesn't carry Echo up the mountain she simply reminds her that every mountain is just steps stacked so.
"A mountain," said Shelly, "is steps stacked just so. Try a small try… then try once more as you go."

The flower at the top of the world 🌸

At the peak smooth, ancient stone, stars close enough to touch grows the flower from the old stories. It blooms in the colours of the choices Echo made to get there.

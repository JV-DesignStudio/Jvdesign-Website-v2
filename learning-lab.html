Quick Reference

Printable Revision Cards

Key facts for each subject. Print the whole section or screenshot individual cards.

💻
Computing
Algorithm & Logic
An algorithm is a step-by-step set of instructions.
A sequence runs instructions one after another.
A loop repeats instructions a set number of times.
Debugging means finding and fixing errors in code.
🔢
Maths — Times Tables
Key Facts
Any number × 10 just adds a zero (e.g. 7 × 10 = 70).
The 5 times table always ends in 0 or 5.
Multiplication is commutative: 3×4 = 4×3.
12 × 12 = 144 the hardest one to memorise!
🌍
Geography
World Knowledge
There are 7 continents: Asia, Africa, N. America, S. America, Antarctica, Europe, Oceania.
Capital of France: Paris. Germany: Berlin. Japan: Tokyo.
The longest river in the world is the Nile (Africa).
The largest country by area is Russia.
🔬
Science
Key Concepts
Gravity pulls objects towards Earth at 9.8 m/s².
Solids, liquids and gases are the three states of matter.
Photosynthesis: plants use sunlight + CO₂ + water to make food.
The food chain starts with a producer (a plant).
🏛️
History
Key Dates
1066: Battle of Hastings Norman conquest of England.
1215: Magna Carta signed by King John.
1939–1945: Second World War.
1969: Neil Armstrong walks on the Moon.
📖
English
Grammar & Spelling
A noun names a person, place or thing.
A verb describes an action or state of being.
i before e except after c receive, believe.
A simile compares using "like" or "as" (e.g. brave as a lion).

# JVDesignStudio Game Curation

The public Games page and JVDS Arcade are storefronts, not a dump of every experiment. New games should be reviewed before they are promoted into the default browse view.

## Statuses

- **Featured**: polished, reliable, distinctive, and representative of the studio. These appear first and form the default Staff Picks view.
- **Complete**: playable and suitable for the public catalogue, but not currently a headline title.
- **Experimental**: an interesting prototype or work-in-progress. It remains discoverable through the Experiments filter, but does not define the first impression.
- **Standalone app**: has its own app identity and must not be bundled into JVDS Arcade.
- **Non-arcade**: a tool, classroom activity, printable project, or other web content. It belongs in its own section.
- **Retired**: broken, redundant, or no longer representative. Keep the source available, but remove it from public browse surfaces.

## Quality review

Score each game from 1 to 5 for fun in the first 30 seconds, mobile controls, visual finish, reliability, replay value, originality, and brand fit. A game needs no individual score below 3 and an average of at least 3.5 to be Complete. Featured games should average at least 4 and include evidence from a desktop and mobile smoke test.

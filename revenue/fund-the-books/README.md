# Fund The Books - BuyMeACoffee Tiers

Turns `buymeacoffee.com/jv_designstudio` into monthly funding for human illustrator.

## Goal Bar (pin to top)
```
🎨 LUMO ART FUND - £0 / £900
Manuscript done. Need £900 for 15 spreads by a human illustrator - No AI.
Every £ goes straight to artist. When we hit £900, I commission.
```

## Tiers (paste into BuyMeACoffee)

**£3/mo - Sketch Supporter**
Thanks for keeping the studio free! Dev Log early access + polls + Thank You wall at /about

**£7/mo - Page Sponsor (RECOMMENDED)**
Most popular. Everything in Supporter + early manuscript PDF + name in Acknowledgements of next book + quarterly Activity Pack (books/activity-pack.html)

**£15/mo - Studio Patron**
Everything in Sponsor + Classroom Kit licence (pages/downloads.html kits for 30 kids) + 50% off all templates + 1 custom character sketch/year

## Why £900
15 spreads @ £60 = £900 (early-career illustrator, rev-share top-up). 40 people @ £7 = £280/mo = book every 3 months.

## Setup
BuyMeACoffee > Membership > Create tiers > Enable Monthly goal > Show on page > Pin post with Lumo pencil sketches.

For Parents and Carers

A safe space to make real games together

JVDesignStudio is a free, ad-free studio where parents and children aged 5+ learn to make games together through step-by-step workshops. No account, no personal data, no cost, ever.

🔒No account required
🚫No ads or tracking
💾No personal data collected
🆓Completely free
📵No social features
🏡Made for home

What your child will learn

🧠
Computational thinking
Breaking problems into steps, spotting patterns, and thinking logically, through building actual games.
🎮
Game design
What makes games fun? Level design, challenge curves, feedback loops. These are real creative skills.
💻
Coding fundamentals
Variables, loops, functions and more, taught in context, not in isolation. Scratch, then GDScript, then real code.
✨
Creative confidence
Every workshop ends with something playable. Seeing your creation work is the best motivation to keep going.

Age ranges

Ages 5-9: Start with the Lap Pad together, then move on to pixel art and Scratch workshops. No typing required, and a grown-up can sit alongside for the youngest makers.

Ages 9-12: Godot and GDevelop workshops. These introduce real code gently with immediate visual results.

Ages 12-16: Roblox, Godot advanced, Unity, C++, proper game engines with scripting and design depth.

Ages 14+: C++, Python, Defold, industry-adjacent tools for young people serious about game dev.

Choosing the right workshop

Every card in the workshop hub shows four labels before you start, so you can match a workshop to your child in under a minute.

Reading
Pictures and short words, short sentences, or some code reading.
Typing
No typing, a few words and numbers, or full code.
Grown-up help
Whether a grown-up is needed for an install or an account.
Software
Browser only, a free app, or a paid game you may already own.

Ages are guidance, not a rule. Each workshop also links back to the previous step and on to the next one, so a learner can move to an easier or harder variation. If a workshop needs an install, the card names the software and shows the browser build when one exists.

Frequently asked questions

Does my child need to create an account?

No. Progress is saved locally in the browser using no personal data. There are no usernames, passwords or email addresses needed to start learning.

Is it really free?

Yes, all workshops are free and always will be. The studio is funded through picture books and games sold on itch.io, not through ads or subscriptions.

What do they need to get started?

Most workshops start with a web browser and nothing else, so they run on a school laptop or a home computer. Some use a free app at home for the full build, and each card says which. The card also shows the reading, typing, grown-up help and software it needs, so you can choose before your child starts.

Can I see what my child is making?

Yes. Many workshops run a live preview beside the steps, and every workshop shows the finished project or an example you can play. You can sit alongside and see the work as it grows. The skill tree shows which quests they have completed.

How do I save progress between sessions?

Progress saves automatically in the browser on the same device. To move to a new device, open the skill tree, tap the 👤 icon, and use "Export Profile" to download a save file, then import it on the new device.

Is there any inappropriate content?

No. All content is designed for young learners. There are no social features, no community chat, and no user-generated content on the site.

## Decision: learn/ vs workshops/ intent split

**learn/** pages are canonical for discovery queries ("Godot for beginners", "Scratch for kids").
**workshops/engine-*.html** pages are canonical for course queries ("Godot workshops", "step-by-step tutorials").
These serve different user intents and should NOT redirect to each other.

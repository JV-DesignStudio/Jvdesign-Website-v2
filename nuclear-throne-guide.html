🎮 What We're Building

A top-down shooter like Nuclear Throne! Your character moves around a arena, shoots at enemies, and tries to survive as long as possible.

🧒 For the Child

You already made a racing car in Zoom Zoom now we're going to make a character that shoots things! It uses the same Godot magic, just with a few new tricks.

👨‍👩‍👧 For the Grown-Up

This workshop builds directly on Zoom Zoom same CharacterBody2D, same move_and_slide(), same scene structure. New concepts introduced: Area2D, signals, instancing, and groups.

✨ What You'll Make

  • A player that moves & aims with the mouse
  • Bullets that fly and hurt enemies
  • Enemies that chase the player
  • Health, score, and a game over screen
  • Randomly generated rooms & corridors
  • An exit door that sends you to the next level
  • Levels that get harder as you go deeper

Accessibility statement

A studio that works for more people

JVDesignStudio is free, browser-first and built for locked-down school computers. We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 AA on the core learner paths, and we publish what works today and what still needs fixing.

Lumo says: if a page is hard to use, tell us. We would much rather fix a barrier than have you struggle through it.

What works today

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, and focus is visible on controls.
  • Launcher and action controls on the main pages meet a 44px touch target.
  • Pages work at 390px on a phone and at 1440px on a desktop without sideways scrolling.
  • Text can be zoomed in the browser, and the studio has a dark theme.
  • Animations respect reduced motion, and the accessibility settings tool adds larger text, high contrast and character voices.
  • Pages use headings, landmarks, labels and a skip link so screen readers can move around.

How we check it

  • Automated keyboard journeys across the landing page, hub, workshop and a creation tool.
  • Contrast checks for colour token pairs and interactive states.
  • Mobile and desktop layout checks at 390px and 1440px, including overflow and tap targets.
  • Accessible-name checks on form controls, buttons and links.
  • Manual spot checks on a school-style laptop and a phone.

Known gaps

  • Some third-party embeds, such as YouTube videos and itch.io games, are outside our control and may not meet the same standard.
  • Canvas games can be played with a keyboard, but full screen-reader parity is not there yet for every game.
  • Some older workshop screenshots still need clearer alt text.
  • Downloadable PDFs and worksheets may be harder to use with a screen reader than the web pages.

If something blocks you

  • Email hello@jvdesignstudio.co.uk with the page name and the device or browser you used.
  • Tell us what you were trying to do and what got in the way. A screenshot helps but is not required.
  • We aim to reply within five working days and to offer a workaround while we fix the issue.
  • Parents and teachers can ask for the same content in another format.

Scope and standard

This statement covers pages and tools that JVDesignStudio builds and controls, including the landing page, Browse, the workshop hub, the flagship quests and the main creative tools. It does not cover third-party sites that we link to or embed.

We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 level AA as our target. The WCAG 2.2 quick reference explains the standard in detail.

If you need this statement or any page in a different format, email us and we will help.

Contact the studio

Email hello@jvdesignstudio.co.uk or use the contact page. You can also read the child safety page for how we keep young creators safe.

Last reviewed: 6 November 2026. We review this statement when a major page or tool changes.

Biscuit Tin Clicker , Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026 · JVDesignStudio

The short version: we don't want your data. Your bakery lives on your device, we don't make you create an account, and the only third party involved is Google, who serves the optional reward adverts.

Who we are

Biscuit Tin Clicker is made by JVDesignStudio, an independent studio in the United Kingdom. You can reach us at joshhyyymakes@gmail.com or via jvdesignstudio.co.uk.

What we collect

Nothing, directly. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no forms. We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, contacts, location, or any other personal information to our own servers, because we don't have any servers.

Your game progress

Your biscuits, helpers, quests, and story progress are saved in local storage on your device only. If you use the Save Transfer feature, the save code is generated on your device and only goes where you choose to paste it. Deleting the app deletes your local save.

Adverts

The app shows optional, reward-based adverts (for example, watching an advert to double your biscuits). These are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure adverts, Google may collect device information such as your advertising identifier. We don't see or store this information ourselves.

  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings at any time.

Children

Biscuit Tin Clicker is a gentle game suitable for all ages. Adverts are never required to play, and we configure advertising to be appropriate for a general audience, including families.

Permissions

The app requests a small, fixed set of permissions and no more:

  • Internet - used solely to load the optional reward adverts. The game itself works fully offline.
  • Vibrate - used for gentle haptic feedback when you tap the tin. Can be turned off in settings.
  • Notifications - optional; only used for the pantry reminders you can switch on yourself. Declined or off by default.
  • Advertising ID - used by Google AdMob to serve and measure the optional adverts (see below). You can reset or delete it in your device's Google settings.

Data deletion

Because everything is stored locally, deleting the app removes all of your data. There is nothing for us to delete on a server, because nothing was ever sent to one.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data (for example, if we add optional cloud saves), we will update this page and the "last updated" date above before the change ships.

Contact

Questions? Email joshhyyymakes@gmail.com and a human will reply.

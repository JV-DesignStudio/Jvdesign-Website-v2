3D Design & Print

What do you want to make?

3D work covers three different jobs. Pick the route that matches the thing you want to finish, then check the software line before you open the first lesson.

Route 1 Create 3D art Model, light and animate a scene, then render it as a still image or an animation. Software: the free Blender app. You install it on a computer. You finish with: a rendered picture or animation. Not a printable model by default. Explore Blender workshops → Route 2 Build 3D objects in the browser Design a rocket, robot, room or game asset right here. No install and no account needed. Software: any modern browser. Nothing to install. You finish with: a 3D model you can export as OBJ, GLB or STL. Pick a browser builder → Route 3 Design for 3D printing Build a measured object, export an STL and check it in a slicer before anything is printed. Software: a browser plus a free TinkerCAD account. A slicer and printer are optional. You finish with: a printable STL file, or a physical print if you have access to a printer. Explore 3D printing workshops →

Rendering is not printing. A Blender render is a picture. To make a physical object you need a printable model (STL), a slicer to check it, and a 3D printer.

The 3D design path

Explore, improve, then invent

Three levels, one goal: finish with a model that is yours. Start wherever you like, but the last level is where you design something new.

Level 1

Explore

Open a guided builder and follow every step. Then change three colours or one part so the build starts to feel like yours.

  • Follow the steps in order.
  • Change three colours or one shape.
  • Export a picture of the build.
Start with Rocket Builder →
Level 2

Improve

Reopen your build or start a design-level one. Change the proportions, add a feature, then say why the design is better.

  • Change one section or add one part.
  • Keep a before and after picture.
  • Write one sentence about why.
Improve a Pirate Ship →
Level 3

Invent

Design something original from a short brief and a few constraints. Export the model, then explain the choices that make it yours.

  • Say who it is for and what it does.
  • Use at least five parts and one part you invented.
  • Export OBJ, GLB or STL and write your design note.

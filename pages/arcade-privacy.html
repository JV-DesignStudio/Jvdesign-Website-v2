JVDS Arcade , Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 July 2026 · JVDesignStudio

The short version: the Arcade collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no adverts, and no analytics. Everything you play and every score you earn stays on your device.

Who we are

JVDS Arcade is made by JVDesignStudio, an independent studio in the United Kingdom. You can reach us at joshhyyymakes@gmail.com or via jvdesignstudio.co.uk.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no forms. We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, contacts, location, or any other personal information, because we have no servers to send it to.

Your game progress

Your profile, XP, level, streak, achievements, and best scores are saved in local storage on your device only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes your progress with it.

Adverts and analytics

The app contains no adverts and no analytics or tracking of any kind. No advertising identifier is read, and no usage data is sent to us or to any third party.

Children

The Arcade is a collection of gentle games suitable for all ages. With no ads, no accounts, and no data collection, there is nothing that would gather information from a child or anyone else.

Permissions and network use

Every game is bundled inside the app and works fully offline. The app does not require your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage.

Data deletion

Because everything is stored locally, deleting the app removes all of your data. There is nothing for us to delete on a server, because nothing was ever sent to one.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data (for example, if we add optional cloud saves in a future version), we will update this page and the "last updated" date above before the change ships.

Contact

Questions? Email joshhyyymakes@gmail.com and a human will reply.

🚀 Your first game in 20 minutes

Make one small thing at each step, then ship it

Draw a character, give it a sound, build a level, then put it all together in the Arcade Game Maker. You can stop after any step and still have made something real.

  1. 1 🎨

    Draw a character

    Pixel Studio. Simple mode for your first sprite, then add a frame or two.

    about 5 min
  2. 2 🎵

    Give it a sound

    Audio Studio. Make one jump or coin sound, or a short loop.

    about 5 min
  3. 3 🧱

    Build a level

    World Builder. Lay out a small space with platforms and hazards.

    about 5 min
  4. 4 🚀

    Ship it and play

    Arcade Game Maker. Drop in your art, sound and level, then play.

    about 5 min

Child safety and safeguarding

A safe place to make things

JVDesignStudio is made for young creators, including children under 13. This page explains how we keep the studio safe, how to report something that feels wrong, and how a parent, guardian or teacher can raise a concern.

Lumo says: if something on any website ever feels wrong, stop and tell a grown-up you trust. You will never be in trouble for asking for help.

Every gallery game is checked

When someone uses Publish, a person reads the submission before it can appear in the Community Gallery. Nothing goes live automatically. If a game is not suitable, it is not published.

The Publish page asks for a grown-up's email so we can reply, and it asks the maker to confirm the work is their own. If the maker says they are under 13, a parent or guardian must add their name and tick the consent box before the form will send.

Reporting a game or a concern

If you see a game that worries you, or something that should not be there, contact us. Include the game title and, if you can, the page link.

  • Email hello@jvdesignstudio.co.uk
  • We remove anything that breaks the gallery rules while we look into it
  • Reports can come from a learner, a parent, a guardian or a teacher

Gallery rules

  • Only share work you made yourself
  • No full names, addresses, school names or other personal details
  • No bullying, hate, scary content aimed at other people, or anything that breaks the law
  • Be kind. A young maker is on the other side of every game

What we store

You do not need an account to learn, make or play. Games, drawings, stories and progress are saved in your own browser on that device, and they stay there unless you export or share them.

We use consent-gated Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, and only after you accept cookies. Newsletter and contact forms send what you type to Formspree. Gallery submissions also go through Formspree and include the maker's first name, a grown-up's email, the game file, and an optional cover and description. We keep them only to review the game and reply, and a parent or guardian can ask us to remove them. The privacy policy lists every service.

For parents, guardians and teachers

  • Children can use the studio with no account, no sign-up and no personal data.
  • The newsletter is for grown-ups, not for children to sign up to alone.
  • You can clear a child's saved work at any time with the browser's site-data controls or the clear-data action in the studio.
  • If a child's email address was sent to us by mistake, email us and we will delete it.
  • Questions about how the studio works in a classroom? Start with the FAQ or contact us.

If you are worried about a child

If a child is in immediate danger, call 999. For advice about a child's safety, contact the NSPCC on 0808 800 5000 or nspcc.org.uk. A young person who needs to talk can contact Childline free on 0800 1111 or childline.org.uk. Reports about online abuse can be made to CEOP.

Contact the studio

Email hello@jvdesignstudio.co.uk for any safety question. A real person reads every message. You can also read the Terms of Service.

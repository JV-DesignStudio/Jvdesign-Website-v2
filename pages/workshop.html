✦ Questions

Before You Start

The things parents, kids and teachers ask most.

Are the workshops really free?

Yes , every course, PDF guide, template and game file on this page is completely free. No account, no email address, no trial. Just click and build.

What ages are these for?

Most workshops suit ages 8+, but the Scratch courses start at 6 and the Godot "My First Video Game" series is designed for ages 5+ with a parent. Each card shows its recommended age.

Do I need to install anything?

The Game Builders, Rocket/Robot builders and JavaScript workshops run entirely in your browser , nothing to install. Engine courses (Godot, Unity, Unreal, Blender) need the free engine installed first, and every course walks you through that setup step by step.

I've never coded before. Is that OK?

That's exactly who these are written for. The My First Series assumes zero experience , every line of code is explained in plain English, with quizzes to check understanding as you go.

Does my progress save?

Yes. Steps, quizzes and XP are saved automatically in your browser, and cards on this page show a "Complete" or "In Progress" badge for anything you've started. Visit My Progress to see everything in one place.

Can teachers use these in class?

Absolutely. The courses work as structured lesson material, there's a ready-made Discovery Session slide deck, and progress tracking works across a shared computer. Everything is free for schools.

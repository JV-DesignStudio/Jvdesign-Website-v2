# SEO Intent Gap Brief - A1770
**Date:** 20 October 2026 | **Status:** Pages 1-3 live

## Why this brief exists
The site ranks well for branded searches but misses high-intent head terms that parents, students and educators type when they are *not* yet looking for JVDesignStudio. This brief identifies 10 pages that cover those gaps without duplicating existing workshop content.

## 10-page plan

| # | Title | Slug | Primary intent | Audience | Internal links |
|---|-------|------|---------------|----------|----------------|
| 1 | How to Make a Game for Free | /pages/how-to-make-a-game.html | Informational / TOFU | 10-16 student | workshop, dev-tools, arcade |
| 2 | Game Design for Kids | /pages/game-design-for-kids.html | Informational + navigational | Parent, educator | workshop, parent-guide, teachers |
| 3 | Free Game Making Tools | /pages/free-game-making-tools.html | Commercial / tool research | 12-17 student | dev-tools, workshop, downloads |
| 4 | Scratch Game Ideas | /pages/scratch-game-ideas.html | Informational | 8-14 student | Scratch workshop, arcade |
| 5 | How to Write a Game Design Document | /pages/game-design-document.html | Informational | 13-18 student | GDD builder tool, workshop |
| 6 | Godot for Beginners | /pages/godot-for-beginners.html | Navigational / tutorial | 14-20 student | Godot workshop series |
| 7 | Pixel Art for Games | /pages/pixel-art-for-games.html | Informational | 10-18 student | Pixel tool, dev-tools |
| 8 | School Game Project Ideas | /pages/school-game-project.html | Informational | Teacher, parent | class.html, workshop, teachers |
| 9 | Game Jam for Beginners | /pages/game-jam-beginner.html | Informational | 14-20 student | workshop, arcade, downloads |
| 10 | Learn Game Development Free | /pages/learn-game-development-free.html | Navigational hub | 13-20 student | workshop, dev-tools, arcade, books |

## Priority order
1. **how-to-make-a-game.html** - highest search volume, broadest intent, feeds all hubs
2. **game-design-for-kids.html** - parent/educator traffic, high conversion to class.html
3. **free-game-making-tools.html** - strongest dev-tools hub tie-in, commercial intent = engaged visitor

Pages 4-10 follow in a second sprint.

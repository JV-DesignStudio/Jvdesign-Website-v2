For parents, carers and teachers

Trust and data, in plain English

JVDesignStudio is a free browser studio for young makers. There are no accounts and no sign-up. A learner can open a tool and start creating straight away. This page shows exactly what the site keeps, where it is kept, and what never leaves the device.

Echo says: knowing where your work lives is part of making it safely. If anything here is unclear, ask us and we will explain it.

Data map

Each row names one thing the site can store, where it goes, how long it stays, and whether it leaves the device.

Creations and saved work

What
Pixel art, characters, levels, sounds, stories and games you save.
Where
Your own browser storage (localStorage) on this device.
How long
Until you clear it, or clear site data in your browser.
Leaves device?
No, unless you export it, share it, or submit it to the gallery.

Progress, XP and badges

What
Workshop progress, XP, streaks and earned badges.
Where
Your own browser storage (localStorage) on this device.
How long
Until you clear it, or clear site data in your browser.
Leaves device?
No. There is no account and no server copy.

Cookie choice

What
Whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies.
Where
Your browser storage on this device.
How long
Until you change it with the Cookie settings link.
Leaves device?
No, other than the analytics described below, and only if you accept.

Newsletter email

What
The email address you type into the newsletter sign-up.
Where
Sent to Formspree, which delivers it to us by email.
How long
Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.
Leaves device?
Yes, only when a grown-up chooses to sign up.

Contact and feedback messages

What
The name, email, role and message you type into a form.
Where
Sent to Formspree, which delivers it to us by email.
How long
Only as long as needed to reply, or until you ask us to delete it.
Leaves device?
Yes, only when someone chooses to send a form.

Community Gallery submission

What
A first name, a grown-up's email, the game file, an optional cover and a short description.
Where
Sent to Formspree, then checked by a person before anything appears.
How long
Until a parent or guardian asks us to remove it.
Leaves device?
Yes, only when someone chooses to submit a game.

Analytics (optional)

What
Pages visited, time on site, browser type and country. Never the content of a creation.
Where
Google Analytics 4, only after you accept cookies.
How long
Aggregated usage reports. No personal profile is built for learners.
Leaves device?
Yes, only after consent. Declining keeps it switched off.

Share links

What
The game or creation encoded into the link itself.
Where
In the web address. We do not store shared content on a server.
How long
As long as the link exists.
Leaves device?
Only if you choose to share the link. is.gd sees the link if you shorten it.

Mobile app extras

What
Optional reward adverts, and a fixed London weather lookup for Cozy Cafe.
Where
Google AdMob and Open-Meteo, inside the apps that offer them.
How long
Only while the app is being used.
Leaves device?
A device advertising ID may go to AdMob. We never see or store it, and location is never requested.

What never leaves the device

Your creations

Drawings, sprites, games, levels, stories and sounds stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to export, share or submit it.

Your progress

XP, badges, streaks and workshop progress are saved locally. There is no account and no server copy to lose or leak.

No adverts on the website

The website shows no adverts and runs no advertising or tracking networks. Analytics is the only measurement, and it waits for consent.

No sign-up to start

A learner can open a tool and make something without giving a name, an email or any personal detail.

How to clear saved work

Because work is stored in the browser, clearing site data removes it from that device. In Chrome, open Settings, then Privacy and security, then Site settings, find this site and clear its data. On a school computer, a teacher may need to do this, or use the browser's clear browsing data option.

You can also change your cookie choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer. Declining analytics never removes saved work.

Getting Started

Classroom Setup in 4 Steps

No IT requests, no installs, no accounts. Students can start in seconds.

1

Open a workshop

Choose a workshop from jvdesignstudio.co.uk/workshop. Filter by engine, subject, or level. Each workshop has a clear goal and time estimate.

2

Students follow the steps

Each workshop guides students through numbered steps with quizzes and challenges along the way. No reading required for youngest learners — just tap and go.

3

Progress saves automatically

XP, streaks, and completion are saved in the browser. Students can pause and resume. No accounts needed — data stays on the device.

4

Print certificates

When a workshop is complete, students can print a certificate showing what they built. Great for displays, portfolios, or parent evenings.

Cozy Cafe Match , Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026 · JVDesignStudio

The short version: we don't want your data. Your cafe lives on your device, we don't make you create an account, and the only third parties involved are Google, who serve the optional reward adverts, and Open-Meteo, who supply the real London weather shown in the cafe window. Neither of them is given anything about you.

Who we are

Cozy Cafe Match is made by JVDesignStudio, an independent studio in the United Kingdom. You can reach us at joshhyyymakes@gmail.com or via jvdesignstudio.co.uk.

What we collect

Nothing, directly. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no forms. We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, contacts, location, or any other personal information to our own servers, because we don't have any servers.

The only outbound requests the app makes are for the optional reward adverts (Google AdMob, see below) and for the local weather shown in the cafe window (see next). Neither request includes your name, email, or device location.

Your game progress

Your coins, cosmetics, achievements, and story progress are saved in local storage on your device only. Deleting the app deletes your local save. Some of that save data (like unlocked hats and trails) is shared with our other JVDesignStudio games, since they use the same on-device save format, but it never leaves your device to do so.

Adverts

The app shows optional, reward-based adverts (for example, watching an advert for a bonus). These are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure adverts, Google may collect device information such as your advertising identifier. We don't see or store this information ourselves.

  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings at any time.

The London weather

To make the cafe feel alive, the app shows the real weather outside, in London, UK. It asks the free Open-Meteo service for the current conditions at a fixed set of London coordinates (51.5074, −0.1278). No location permission is used and your own location is never requested, read, or sent. The request contains no account, identifier, or personal data about you. Like any web request, Open-Meteo will see the IP address it is connecting from; the result is cached on your device for offline use.

  • Open-Meteo privacy: open-meteo.com/en/terms

If you are offline, the cafe simply uses the last weather it saw (or a mild default), and everything else plays exactly the same.

Children

Cozy Cafe Match is a gentle game suitable for all ages. There are no fail states and no pressure; the cafe day is a relaxed session that pauses whenever you put the game down. Adverts are never required to play, and we configure advertising to be appropriate for a general audience, including families.

Permissions

The app requests the Internet permission (used to load the optional adverts and the London weather) and Vibrate (used for gentle haptic feedback when you match tiles). No location permission is requested. Everything else, including your save and the whole game, works offline.

Data deletion

Because everything is stored locally, deleting the app removes all of your data. There is nothing for us to delete on a server, because nothing was ever sent to one.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data (for example, if we add optional cloud saves), we will update this page and the "last updated" date above before the change ships.

Publish your game

Made something with the Arcade Game Maker? Send it in here. A person checks every submission before it goes in the gallery - nothing goes live automatically.

How it works
  1. Build your game in the Arcade Game Maker.
  2. Fill in the form below and attach the game file and an optional cover image.
  3. A human at JVDesignStudio reviews every submission for safety and quality.
  4. If approved, your game appears in the community gallery with your chosen display name.

How old are you?

Parent or guardian details

Because you are under 13, a parent or guardian must add their name and agree before your game can be considered.

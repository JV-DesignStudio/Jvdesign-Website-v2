How Learning Tracks Work

📋
Follow a Path
Each track is a curated sequence of workshops and games. Start at step 1, work through to the end.
🛠️
Build, Then Play
Most tracks alternate between making a game in a workshop, then playing the finished version. Learn by doing.
🎮
Play the Games
Every track includes browser games you can play right now. No installs, no accounts — just open and go.
📊
Track Progress
Your XP, streaks and completion status are saved in your browser automatically. Come back anytime and pick up where you left off.
🔀
Mix and Match
Tracks are suggestions, not rules. Jump between them, try different ages, come back to favourites. It's your pace.
🆕
Always Growing
New workshops, games and tracks are added regularly. Bookmark the hub and check back whenever you like.

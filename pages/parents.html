Safety & Privacy

Built With Kids in Mind

Every design decision prioritises child safety. Here's exactly what we do — and don't do.

🔒

No Accounts

No login, no password, nothing to create. Kids can start learning the moment the page loads.

💬

No Chat, No Comments

Children cannot contact each other on this site. Zero social features means zero risk.

📦

All Progress Stays Here

Everything is stored in your browser's localStorage. No server copy. Export a backup file when you need to switch devices.

📊

Analytics Only If You Accept

Google Analytics loads only after the cookie banner's Accept. Decline or ignore = no tracking at all.

🎮

No Advertising

The website never shows ads. Apps may show ads, but jvdesignstudio.co.uk is completely ad-free.

✨

External Links Are Labeled

Scratch, Roblox Studio, itch.io links open in a new tab. We clearly label them as leaving our site.

UK GDPR & Age Appropriate Design Code

Withdrawal is as easy as consent: footer → Cookie settings clears the choice and re-shows the banner. No personal data beyond analytics (if consented) is collected. Contact for data questions: Contact page.

External Sites & Consent

Workshop pages that link to external sites (Scratch, Roblox Studio, itch.io) now include the cookie banner. Cookies are set only after Accept. Decline keeps analytics_storage: denied. You can manage settings anytime via the footer.

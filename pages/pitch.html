✦ What I do

Digital things that help children with big feelings.

I'm one person building books, games, courses and creative tools that turn emotional wellbeing into something kids can hold, play and learn from. It started as an author with a laptop and no framework. Today it is a 100+ page platform that is free to use, safe by design, and made entirely in-house.

Why it's different
  • 💜Emotional first. The stories name the hard things honestly and gently, then games and lessons let kids explore them by doing.
  • 🔒Safe and free by design. No ads, no accounts, no data taken from a single child. Just open it and go.
  • 🔨Handmade craft. A 100+ page platform with zero frameworks or templates. Quality you can feel in every corner.
  • 🌍One creator, wide reach. Writing, art, code, design and teaching, all under one roof and moving fast.

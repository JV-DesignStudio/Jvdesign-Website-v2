Visitor-to-Maker Funnel

Weekly check - which hub converts, which CTA is wasted. Pull from GA4 each Monday.

The funnel

1
Arrives at a hub arrival_hub
Visitor lands on Learn, Create, Play or Read hub. Fired once per session per hub.
Which hub gets the most arrivals? Which gets almost none?
2
Starts playing a game play_start
First interaction (click or key) on any page under /games/.
What share of hub arrivals end up playing something?
3
Starts making something first_create_start
First interaction on a tool or workshop page. The maker journey begins here.
What share of visitors move from play to create?
4
Saves or exports first_create_complete key drop
Downloads a file, clicks a save button, or the page dispatches jvds:created. This is the creation moment.
What share of makers finish something? This is the main conversion number.
5
Shares a creation first_create_share
Uses the native share sheet or a share button. Not available on all tools.
What share of completions lead to sharing? Low is expected - not a CTA problem.
6
Returns return_visit
Visitor who has been before (detected via localStorage flag). Fired once per session.
What share of sessions are return visits? Rising trend means the site is sticking.

How to pull the report in GA4

Go to Explore → Blank in GA4 and build a funnel exploration, or use Reports → Engagement → Events and filter by the event names below.

Weekly baseline questions:
1. Did the Create hub arrival count go up or down vs last week?
2. What is the first_create_start to first_create_complete ratio? Target: above 30%.
3. Which tool page has the biggest drop between start and complete? That is the CTA to fix.
4. Is the return visit share rising? If not, check if the "Continue where you left off" strip is working.

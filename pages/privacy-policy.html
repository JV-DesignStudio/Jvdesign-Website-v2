Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Short version: We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how people use the site, but only if you accept cookies. There are no accounts and no sign-up. Game saves stay in your browser. The newsletter asks for your email and nothing else. Echo says: your creations stay yours.

1. What we collect

Site usage (pages visited, time on site, browser type, country) through Google Analytics (GA4), only if you accept cookies. Your newsletter email address through Formspree, only if you subscribe. We have no accounts, no sign-up, and no server-side storage of your games or creations.

2. Local storage

Your games, settings, and progress are saved in your browser's localStorage and never leave your device unless you export or share them.

3. Google Analytics (GA4)

We use Google Analytics (GA4) with anonymised data and consent mode that stays denied until you choose. You can accept or decline when the banner appears, and change your mind anytime with the Cookie settings link in the footer. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

4. Newsletter (Formspree)

The footer newsletter form sends the email address you type to Formspree, which delivers it to us so we can send studio news (about one email a week). That address is used only for the newsletter. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to delete it at the address below. Formspree's privacy policy: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy

5. Shared / exported content

Share links encode your game or creation into the URL; we don't store shared content on any server. Exported HTML files stay on your device only. Shortened share links go through is.gd, which sees the link you shorten.

6. Third-party services on this site

  • Google Fonts, fonts from the Google CDN
  • Google Analytics and Tag Manager (GA4), only after you accept cookies
  • unpkg, web-vitals measurement script, only after you accept cookies
  • jsDelivr, Phaser game engine CDN
  • is.gd, URL shortener for share links
  • Formspree, newsletter signup delivery

Outbound links (Instagram, YouTube, itch.io, Buy Me a Coffee) take you to other sites with their own privacy policies. Donation or merch pages never run inside our site.

7. Mobile apps and adverts

This website itself shows no adverts. Some of our mobile apps offer optional reward adverts served by Google AdMob (Biscuit Tin Clicker, Cozy Cafe Match, Sky High With Friends). Google may collect device information such as your advertising identifier for those adverts; we never see or store it. Cozy Cafe Match also asks the Open-Meteo weather service for fixed London coordinates so the cafe window shows real weather; your location is never requested or sent. QuestLog, JVDS Arcade, Game Maker and Pocket Crew contain no adverts and no analytics. Each app has its own policy page with the same statements: Biscuit Tin, Cozy Cafe, Sky High, QuestLog, Arcade, Game Maker, Pocket Crew.

8. Children and parental consent

JVDesignStudio is made for young creators, including children under 13. We do not require any personal data to learn, create or play: no accounts, no names, no required tracking. Children should use the site with a parent, guardian or teacher. The newsletter is meant for grown-ups (parents and teachers), not for children to sign up alone. If a child's email address was submitted by mistake, a parent or guardian can email us and we will delete it. For the apps, each per-app policy above includes its own children section with the same promise.

9. Your rights and contact

You can ask what data we hold about you, ask us to delete your newsletter address, or withdraw cookie consent anytime through the Cookie settings footer link. Questions? Email hello@jvdesignstudio.co.uk

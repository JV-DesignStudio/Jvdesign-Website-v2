Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Short version: We use Google Analytics to understand how people use the Arcade Game Maker. We don't sell your data. All game saves stay in your browser. You can opt out at any time.

1. What we collect

Usage analytics, pages visited, time on site, browser type, country. Collected via Google Analytics only if you consent. We have no accounts, no sign-up, no server-side storage of your games.

2. Local storage

Your games, settings, and progress are saved in your browser's localStorage and never leave your device unless you export or share them.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) with anonymised data. You can decline when the banner appears. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

4. Shared / exported games

Share links encode your game into the URL, we don't store shared games on any server. Exported HTML files stay on your device only.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Fonts, fonts from Google CDN
  • jsDelivr, Phaser game engine CDN
  • is.gd, URL shortener for share links

6. Contact

Questions? Email hello@jvdesignstudio.co.uk

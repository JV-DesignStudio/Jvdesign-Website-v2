## The three stages

**Beginner**: Makes a small, complete thing by following a guided step. Support: Every step is named, one concept at a time, with a check before moving on. Evidence: A first playable build, saved file or finished project the learner can show.
**Builder**: Combines the beginner ideas into a bigger project and makes at least one choice of their own. Support: The goal and the checkpoints are given; the learner chooses some parts and fixes small mistakes. Evidence: An extended project with one original feature, plus a short note on what changed.
**Creator**: Plans a project from a brief, builds it independently and debugs it without a step-by-step guide. Support: Only the outcome, constraints and reference material are given. Evidence: A complete project, a design note and a shared or exported build.

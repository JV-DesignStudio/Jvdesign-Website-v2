Short Bio (50 words)

J. Valentine is a UK-based children's author and designer behind JVDesignStudio. Their work spans picture books, tabletop games, and digital experiences — all crafted with the belief that brave doesn't always have to be loud, and that every heart has a spark that can never be dimmed.

Extended Bio (100 words)

J. Valentine is the author and designer behind JVDesignStudio — a creative studio making children's picture books, tabletop games, and digital experiences. Their books explore themes of resilience, curiosity, kindness, and quiet courage, including the widely shared Lumo and the Grumble-Grit, a story about carrying invisible struggles with patience and strength.

After years of learning how to carry their own "Grumble-Grit," J. Valentine creates stories that don't try to fix hard things — they sit with them honestly, and show young readers that a different kind of strength is still strength. JVDesignStudio is based in the United Kingdom.

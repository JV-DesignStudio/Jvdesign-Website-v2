# Game Cover Art Briefs

Each cover is **480×270px (16:9)**, saved as `.webp` into `assets/game-thumbs/`.
They appear as arcade cabinet images  -  the first thing a player sees before clicking Play.

**Design rule:** One clear character or action. No text. Strong silhouette. Should read at 120px wide.

---

## 1. Echo's Swim
**File:** `assets/game-thumbs/echo-flight.webp`  
**Character:** Echo (axolotl)  
**Palette:** Teal/cyan underwater  -  deep ocean blues with warm coral gate glow

**Scene:** Echo mid-swim between two glowing coral gates. Bubbles trail behind. A gap just wide enough to fit through. Sense of speed, slight upward angle. Background is dark deep water fading to lighter teal near the surface.

**Mood:** Focused, playful. Like a single breath of concentration.

## 4. Tower Defence (Hearthguard)

---

## Notes for all covers

- Keep characters consistent with their character sheet art
- Avoid any text/UI elements in the cover image
- Aim for ~2 - 3 clear focal points maximum
- Dark backgrounds read better at small arcade-card sizes
- Export at 480×270px, compressed `.webp` (target <60KB each)

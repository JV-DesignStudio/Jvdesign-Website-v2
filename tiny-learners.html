Tiny Learners

Draw, count, colour, and play your way through ten fun adventures with Echo, Pip and friends. Made for little hands No typing needed!

🌟 Ages 4–6 🎨 Draw & Create 🆓 Always Free 📱 Works on Tablet

👋 For Parents & Carers

What your child is learning

Every activity is built around early years learning goals. No reading, typing or prior knowledge needed just tap, look and listen.

🔢
Number & Counting
One-to-one correspondence, number recognition and simple addition using visual objects.
Maths Foundation
🔤
Phonics & Letters
Letter sounds (phonemes), initial letter recognition, and matching sounds to words.
Early Reading
🔵
Shapes & Colours
Naming common 2D shapes, recognising colours, and basic visual discrimination.
EYFS Core
📐
Size & Comparison
Bigger/smaller, taller/shorter — building the vocabulary of measurement and comparison.
Maths Language
🔁
Patterns & Sequences
Spotting repeating patterns and ordering events — foundational for maths and reading.
Pattern Recognition
🎨
Creative Expression
Free drawing develops fine motor skills, hand-eye coordination and self-expression.
Creative Development
🗂️
Sorting & Categories
Finding the odd one out builds logical thinking and vocabulary for classifying the world.
Critical Thinking
🎵
Rhyme & Language
Recognising rhyming words supports phonological awareness a key pre-reading skill.
Literacy Foundation

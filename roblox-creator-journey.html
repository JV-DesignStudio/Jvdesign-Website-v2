🎮 Free Interactive Course

Build Your Own
Roblox Cosmetics

A step-by-step interactive guide for ages 12+. Learn to create t-shirts, clothing, and 3D accessories using free tools — no experience needed.

🌐

Browser-Based

All tools run in your browser. No downloads, no installations. Works on any computer.

📺

Screen-Share Ready

Designed for live sessions. Teacher watches via screen share and guides the student through each step.

🏆

Level Up System

6 levels from beginner to advanced. Earn XP, unlock new challenges, and track your progress.

💾

Saves Progress

Your progress is saved automatically. Close the tab and come back anytime — you won't lose a thing.

Built for students and teachers. Uses Onshape (3D modelling), GIMP/Pixlr (image editing), and Roblox Studio.
